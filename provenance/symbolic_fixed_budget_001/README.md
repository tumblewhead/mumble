# Stop growing individual bases; constrain the joint approximation

The user's concern is supported by the symbolic analysis. The latest rest repair
is a valid diagnostic improvement on that fixture, but its larger independent
free/forced bases are not a satisfactory architectural direction by themselves.
They generally break a physical complement identity. More coefficients also cost
quadratically in the joint factor. Freeze further basis growth and judge a whole
representation under one joint error contract and a fixed memory ceiling.

No estimator, kernel, solver, prior, support or campaign default changed here.
This is a symbolic reassessment and small diagnostic witness, not another refit.
Previous results and their source hashes remain intact. Base 3f2da5e, branch
experiment/symbolic-fixed-budget-001. Three individually reviewed proofs:
[J1](../../specifications/symbolic_fixed_budget_001/J1.md),
[K1](../../specifications/symbolic_fixed_budget_001/K1.md),
[B1](../../specifications/symbolic_fixed_budget_001/B1.md).

## Why error moves between parameters

Let r be the whitened direct voltage residual and e its compression defect, with
J=Dr and E=De. The exact likelihood-gradient change is

    Delta g = J^T e + E^T r + E^T e.

It is this joint vector, filtered through the full curvature, that moves a fit.
Small voltage error alone is insufficient: derivative error E can interact with
the observed residual, and parameter correlations spread the displacement. A
physical partition can isolate the origin of error without isolating its effects.
Repairing one error component can remove cancellation with another.

In the affine/quadratic case, with P the direct precision, define
A=P^-1/2(Phat-P)P^-1/2 and b=P^-1/2 Delta g at the direct optimum. Then exactly

    P^1/2(thetahat-theta*) = -(I+A)^-1 b.

For nonlinear fits this is a local surrogate, not an exact displacement theorem;
the exact Hessian includes residual-weighted second derivatives. J1 separates the
two. A query's mean and covariance additionally depend on its state-transport
map and derivative. Evaluate score, precision and query transport together.
Coordinate relabeling leaves these local physical quantities invariant; a new
coordinate is useful only if it changes approximation quality or conditioning.

## A structural defect in the latest partition

The free response F and forced response u are generated by one RC kernel. Under
constant current I,

    u=I(1-F),    v=RI+(b-RI)F.

Consequently an initially equilibrated branch b=RI must stay at v=RI for every
tau. Its tau sensitivity must also be zero. If free and forced approximations use
different functions Ff and Fu, the error becomes

    vhat-v = (b-RI)(Ff-F) + RI(Ff-Fu).

The second term is a consistency defect. It survives even when b=RI. The mixed
free5/forcedTaylor2 repair has precisely this possibility. Its all-rest success
could not reveal it because I=0. This extends the earlier O1 equilibrium-preservation
principle to the representation itself, rather than merely rounded recurrence
coefficients.

The [finite native witness](check.json) uses 512 public irregular-interval samples,
I=120 mA, public R=(.035,.07,.1) ohm, b=RI, 25 C and three common log-tau offsets.
It runs existing pinned kernels, evaluates only the RC contribution, and performs
no fit or truth-based initialization of an estimator.

| log-tau offset | Mixed free5/forced2 maximum total RC variation (uV) | Same-family Taylor2 / collocation5 |
|---|---:|---:|
| -0.9 | 566.498 | rounding |
| 0 | 3.569 | rounding |
| +0.9 | 568.802 | rounding |

Mixed maximum branch log-tau sensitivity at +0.9 is about 1921 uV per unit log-tau,
where the physical equilibrium sensitivity is zero. Same-family total voltage
identity errors are below 7e-11 uV in these checks. These small floating values test
the identity, not real battery accuracy. The witness demonstrates an inconsistency;
it does not prove that it caused any particular prior active-fit regression.

Shared fixed-rate realizations or shared constant-reproducing approximation
operators can preserve the identity with bounded response state. They do not
automatically halve history storage: arbitrary input makes free and forced
coefficient histories distinct, and the joint likelihood still contains their
cross terms. Signed interpolation weights also do not automatically preserve
passivity or positivity. Closure, shape accuracy and useful bounds must be shown.

## Why memory and long duration need explicit contracts

The packed factor grew from 4760 B to 7568 B (W34→43): nine added features cost 2808 B
because every feature couples to the rest. Padded state added another 144 B, raising
the partial subtotal from 5168 B to 8120 B. The new symbolic work adds zero persistent
estimator bytes. Neither subtotal includes full prior/solver/workspace/stack costs.

B1 shows, with a retained Gaussian prior and arbitrary fresh observations, that a
constant response bias can produce a nearly constant fit shift while uncertainty
shrinks as 1/sqrt(N). Correct local precision therefore does not protect trust over
long use. Its separate Vandermonde argument shows why an arbitrary continuous-rate
rest likelihood cannot generally be retained exactly by fewer fixed linear data
moments plus a squared-data normalizer. This limited result does not rule out
useful approximate, adaptive or nonlinear compression.

Current physical state can forget an initial transient while evidence from that
transient remains informative. State contraction does not justify dropping its
likelihood. A physically specified aging law may change old evidence's relevance;
an invented decay or noise inflation cannot supply that law.

## Revised direction

1. Freeze basis growth. Use the previous W≤34 / 4760 B factor as a fixed ceiling
   for the next desktop comparison. Keep the approximately 10 KiB goal for the entire
   deployed subsystem, including peak workspace; no complete allocation is proved.
2. Derive candidates from shared physical response operators. Require complement,
   equilibrium and their derivative identities, chronological carried state and
   one ownership of evidence before optimizing approximation accuracy.
3. Select and evaluate representations using joint score, relative precision,
   query mean and transported uncertainty across the original support. Q/SOC/load
   tolerances guide utility; nuisance uncertainty and cross terms remain explicit.
   Evaluate feasible replacements at equal storage, not isolated degree increases.
4. Require a cumulative error/evolution argument before indefinite operation. A
   local precision pass or six good cases cannot substitute. If the declared
   support/query contract cannot fit the budget, report that conflict rather than
   silently adding rank, narrowing support or forgetting evidence.

This is a proposed redesign contract, not a selected reduced model or a promised
memory saving. The larger free5/forced2 study stays as a diagnostic benchmark;
it should not advance toward production while the consistency defect remains.
No candidate satisfying the full fixed-budget contract is claimed yet.
