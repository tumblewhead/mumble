# P1: physical constraints on a shared three-state RC operator

Scope: exact arithmetic, stationary positive tau_c and R, original physical
query t=log(tau/tau_c) in [-1,1], piecewise constant bounded current with actual
ordered intervals. Age below means elapsed response time, not cell degradation.
The reference is the same Foster branch as K1/C1. Neither variable temperature
nor drifting unknown parameters is covered by this stationary theorem.

## A closed family, without increasing history rank

Put a=1/tau_c, d=a*age. The existing three centre jets span

    Fhat(d;t) = exp(-d) [1 + alpha(t)*d + beta(t)*d^2].

For any twice continuously differentiable alpha,beta use the SAME functional

    L_t f = f(0) + (alpha+beta)*f_t(0) + beta*f_tt(0)

on the free and forced centre jets. Here derivatives of f are with respect to
log tau at its fixed original centre. Since f_t=d exp(-d) and
f_tt=(d^2-d)exp(-d) for the free response, this gives the displayed polynomial.
The existing chronological differentiated recurrence closes these jets; L is
linear, reproduces constants, and commutes with the finite input-step sum.
Thus Fhat(0;t)=1, uhat=I(1-Fhat) for constant I, and for arbitrary input
uhat=khat*I with khat=-dFhat/dage. All six free/forced states per branch remain
necessary. Never propagate only the weighted endpoint or reset forced state at rest.
Changing a physical query evaluates the same retained stationary hypothesis;
it is not an evolution law for an aging battery.

Central value/slope/curvature matching for all ages is equivalent to

    alpha(0)=beta(0)=0,
    alpha'(0)=1, beta'(0)=0,
    alpha''(0)=-1, beta''(0)=1.

Proof: equate coefficients of 1,d,d^2 in the three central derivatives.
Query derivative weights are alpha'+beta' and beta'; second derivatives likewise.
They must enter the full physical coordinate chain, including ordered tau gaps.
The family uses the existing W34 factor with the existing H Taylor features.
Choosing functions is still open. Their executable code/constants, derivative
work, and rounding must be counted; constant feature count is not free compute.

## Necessary and sufficient positivity conditions in this family

For a real quadratic A+B*d+C*d^2 on d>=0, nonnegativity is equivalent to

    A>=0, C>=0, and [B>=0 OR B^2<=4*A*C].                 (Q)

Proof: C<0 fails at infinity and A<0 fails at zero. If B>=0 the minimum on the
half-line is at zero. If B<0 and C>0, the minimum is A-B^2/(4C).
C=0,B<0 fails at infinity; (Q) also excludes it. This includes equality cases.

Here khat/a = exp(-d) [(1-alpha)+(alpha-2*beta)*d+beta*d^2].
Therefore imposing (Q) with A=1-alpha,B=alpha-2*beta,C=beta for every t is
necessary and sufficient for khat>=0 at all ages. Because Fhat starts at1 and
ends at0, integration then proves 0<=Fhat<=1, monotone relaxation, unit integral
of khat, and |uhat|<=Imax for arbitrary bounded signed input from zero state.
These are exact structural constraints, not sampled penalties or error tolerances.
Bounded input response is not a likelihood accuracy or covariance certificate.

## Separate driven-response energy condition

Nonnegative impulse response alone does not imply dissipativity. Let z be the
Laplace variable. Direct integration yields

    Ghat(z) = (1-alpha)/(1+z/a)
            + (alpha-2*beta)/(1+z/a)^2 + 2*beta/(1+z/a)^3.

Its real part on z=i*omega, writing y=(omega/a)^2, is

    Re Ghat = [1 + (2-2*alpha-6*beta)*y
                 + (1-2*alpha+2*beta)*y^2] / (1+y)^3.

Expansion of the three fractions proves this identity. Consequently Re Ghat>=0
at every frequency iff (Q) holds for A=1,B=2-2*alpha-6*beta,
C=1-2*alpha+2*beta. We require BOTH sets of inequalities.
For real square-integrable input and zero initial state, the stable convolution
and Fourier inner-product identity give integral I*(R*uhat) dt >=0 when R>0.
This is a zero-state energy statement. It does not construct a storage function
for arbitrary auxiliary initial states, prove complete monotonicity of Fhat,
or establish whole-cell electrochemical passivity. Treat those as separate
claims if later needed. An initial scalar b uses b*Fhat, not arbitrary jet states.

The constraints are locally compatible with centre matching: alpha=t-t^2/2,
beta=t^2/2 have the required jets; for sufficiently small |t| the impulse
quadratic has discriminant condition margin t^2+O(t^3) when its linear term is
negative, and the energy coefficients remain positive near their centre2,1.
This proves a local nonempty feasible neighbourhood, not a full-support fit.
The earlier Cayley map alpha=s-s^2/2,beta=s^2/2 at t=-1 violates the impulse
condition and is rejected before inference. Sharing a kernel alone was insufficient.

## A useful limitation on an alternative positive exponential bank

Suppose fixed distinct positive rates lambda_j and C1 nonnegative query weights
w_j(t) sum to1 on an open neighbourhood of t=0. If sum_j w_j(0) exp(-lambda_j*u)
is exactly exp(-lambda_c*u) for every u>=0, derivatives at u=0 give weighted
mean lambda_c and second moment lambda_c^2. The weighted variance is zero, so
all positive mass is on lambda_c (if absent, equality is impossible).
Every zero weight has a local minimum at0, hence w_j'(0)=0. The one active
weight also has derivative zero because the sum stays1. Thus the query
sensitivity of the entire bank is zero at0, whereas the physical log-tau
sensitivity is lambda_c*u*exp(-lambda_c*u), nonzero for u>0.
A smooth finite positive-weight bank cannot have both exact centre response
and exact centre sensitivity at an interior centre. This is not an impossibility
for signed realizations, repeated poles, approximate centre matching, adaptive
states or general positive systems. It stops an unjustified switch to convex
weights as a universally free repair.

## Error and evolution obligations retained

For E0=sup_age|Fhat-F|, E1=sup_age|partial_t(Fhat-F)| and
K0=integral|khat-k|, K1=integral|partial_t(khat-k)|, convolution gives

    |vhat-v| <= |b| E0 + R Imax K0,
    |partial_t(vhat-v)| <= |b| E1 + R Imax K1.

Compact t support and bounded C1 coefficients give finite bounds because all
rates are positive and exponential-polynomial derivatives integrably dominate.
No useful numeric bound is claimed. Uniform proof over support, full joint
score/precision/query criteria in P2, H error, and accumulated/lifetime error
remain required. No physical support narrowing, centre migration, exponentials
that forget evidence, or degree increase follows from this theorem.
