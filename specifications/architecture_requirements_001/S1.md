# S1: joint boundary-message semantics

This is an exact reference identity, not a finite-storage or aging-model proof.
It adds two distinct types without redefining the prior-free EvidenceObject in
architecture_contracts_001/interfaces.json.

Let b_k contain dynamic state, reference health, static unknowns and any finite
memory required by the declared transition/measurement law. Assume that, conditional
on b_k and the declared input/action history, the law of the next boundary and
observation depends on no earlier hidden state. Include policy/selection effects
and uncertain inputs in this law; conditioning noisy measured current as if exact
does not satisfy the premise for a different actual law. The state space and
reference measures are fixed, or qualified coordinate transports are supplied.

Define a nonnegative transition-evidence kernel K_k(b,db') for exactly one owned
interval: physical transition together with that interval's observation likelihood
under its sensor model. It may be substochastic or unnormalized as a likelihood;
it is not a posterior and contains no initialization prior. A density notation
K_k=f_k(db'|b,u_k)*g_k(y_k|b,b',u_k) is permitted when this factorization is valid.
A joint kernel is needed otherwise. Deterministic state transitions are allowed
as measures without pretending their Dirac masses are Lebesgue densities.

Let pi_0 be the original public joint prior as a measure. Assume all the following
integrals are well defined, finite, and have positive total mass when normalization
is requested. The unnormalized boundary measure is

    mu_0 = pi_0
    mu_(k+1)(A) = integral K_k(b,A) mu_k(db).

`TransitionEvidence` owns K on a disjoint chronological interval, its domain,
input/measurement semantics, transport, and approximation ledger.
`BoundaryMessage` owns mu and its prior identity, entire ancestor prefix, physical
separator, measure/support and cumulative approximation ledger. A normalized
publication is mu/mu(whole space), with any required scale kept separately.
The original prior is already included in mu. Neither mu nor its local Gaussian
view can also be inserted as fresh independent evidence beside its ancestors.

## Exact composition lemma

For consecutive intervals define

    (K_a K_b)(b,A) = integral K_b(c,A) K_a(b,dc).

Nonnegativity permits exchanging the integration order (Tonelli). Therefore
`(mu K_a) K_b = mu (K_a K_b)`. Induction shows that the final boundary measure
equals the original prior multiplied by all transition/observation factors and
integrated over internal boundaries, in their original order. Every observation
factor and the prior occur exactly once. Parenthesization changes the grouping,
not the chronological order. The conditional-independence premise makes b_k a
sufficient separator for subsequent inference under this model.

This proves the exact semantic target for a history compactor. It does not imply
that the kernel/message can be represented with a finite number of coefficients,
that the integrals can be efficiently evaluated, that a local fit equals them,
or that the model has calibrated real-cell coverage. Approximation must separately
satisfy A6, and numerical grouping need not be associative in floating point.
Likelihood-scaled kernels are not automatically contraction operators after
normalization; accumulated approximation needs a new bound, not the associativity
identity. Prior-free block composition is distinct from combining posteriors.

## Set semantics are a different type

If no probabilistic evolution law is justified, a declared relation R_k and
measurement-admissibility relation Y_k can define

    C_(k+1) = outer({b': exists b in C_k, (b,b') in R_k intersect Y_k}).

Here the intersection is over pairs on the same domain; a measurement relation
may depend on both endpoints. If the actual pair belongs to both relations and
the actual current state is in C_k, the successor is in the displayed set, hence
in its outer representation. Induction is A7 containment. This is not a probability
measure and receives neither normalized likelihood weights nor posterior labels.
Use distinct types/guarantee labels even if both encode geometry with a codebook.

## Boundary failures and obligations

For stationary parameters the transition preserves those parameters exactly and
advances only the dynamic state; existing stationary sufficient-factor controls
can be compared to this reference in their restricted family. With aging, old
measurements constrain earlier health and reach current health only through K/R.
Two paths with the same final Q generally have different charge integrals, so a
health centroid alone cannot replace the separator/path relation in general.

If future behavior depends on omitted exposure, thermal history, calibration drift,
or latent spline coefficients, the conditional-independence premise fails. Augment
b or mark the profile unsupported. No claim is made that such augmentation closes
in a small space. Data-dependent parameter updates must not be recast as a new
physical transition independent of the same observations. An approximate mu may
be used as a carried message only with its ancestry and accumulated error attached.

Required witnesses for implementation acceptance: disjoint regrouping against a
retained-data reference; duplicate/prior injection rejection; deterministic and
probabilistic transitions with proper measures; changing-Q paths with identical
endpoints; correlated sensor-state augmentation; reset/gap and domain-migration
failures. They are obligations, not tests executed in this documentation step.
