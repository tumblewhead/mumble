# B1: bounded memory needs an approximation contract, not endless refinement

This makes explicit two limits behind symbolic_system_001/U1,A1 and
rc_remainder_bounds_001/P3b. All statements below are conditional; they do not
claim that the battery estimator is impossible or that fixed memory cannot give
useful approximate inference.

## Constant systematic bias survives while statistical width shrinks

Consider N scalar observations with normalized Gaussian residual r_i(theta)=theta-y_i,
fixed Gaussian prior precision lambda>0 and prior mean0. Replace the response by
theta+delta for a fixed nonzero delta. Direct minimization gives

    theta*=sum_i y_i/(N+lambda),
    thetahat=theta* - N*delta/(N+lambda),
    SD=SDhat=1/sqrt(N+lambda).

Thus the shift in reference SD units is N*|delta|/sqrt(N+lambda), unbounded as
N grows, although the response defect is constant and the precision agrees
exactly. This holds for arbitrary realized y_i, including fresh observations;
no repeated-noise assumption or removal of the prior is required. It is a
counterexample to universal lifetime fidelity from bounded response error plus
correct local covariance, not a prediction of an actual Foster trajectory.
Correlated likelihoods or a qualified aging law change the calculation, but may
not be invented as a remedy for deterministic approximation bias.

## Exact fixed-rank linear voltage-history summaries have a limited domain

Restrict to the rest submodel y_i=b exp(-lambda t_i)+noise, with known noise scale,
distinct positive t_i and unknown nonzero b and lambda in an open positive
interval. The data-dependent objective contains

    -b sum_i y_i exp(-lambda t_i) + (sum_i y_i^2)/2.

Suppose the saved data-dependent summary is Ay and ||y||^2, where A has m<N rows,
is fixed independently of y, and can depend on the known times. There is nonzero
v in null(A), so data y=v and y=-v have the same summary. Their objectives differ
by -2b sum_i v_i exp(-lambda t_i).

The functions exp(-lambda t_i) are linearly independent on any open lambda
interval. Proof: if their linear combination vanishes there, take derivatives
of orders0..N-1 at an interior lambda0. The resulting matrix is a Vandermonde in
the distinct -t_i times nonzero diagonal exp(-lambda0 t_i), and is invertible.
Hence all coefficients vanish, contradicting v!=0. For nonzero v the sum is not
constant on the interval either: differentiate it once and use t_i>0 and the same
independence. Thus the two objectives cannot differ only by a parameter-independent
normalizer, and no reconstruction from this summary recovers both exact continuous
likelihoods. The known data-independent terms do not repair that ambiguity.

This applies to fixed-basis linear cross moments plus a squared-data normalizer,
including the usual finite lifted least-squares factor with data-independent
response features. It does not cover arbitrary nonlinear/data-adaptive encodings,
unbounded-precision digit tricks, a finite parameter grid or approximate summaries.
It does not prove a particular approximation lower bound in bytes. Low-dimensional
battery parameters do not by themselves supply finite exact global sufficient
statistics for arbitrary nonlinear observation histories.

## Memory arithmetic and admissible next questions

A dense packed double factor with W lifted features costs4W(W+1) bytes. Increasing
W by k costs4k(2W+k+1), quadratically coupling a local basis addition to all other
features through the retained cross terms. The last change W34->43 costs2808 B
in the factor alone, before144 B additional padded response state. This explains
why repairing one response independently can consume disproportionate memory.

Free response F(t) decays, but old likelihood terms such as sums of y_i F(t_i)
do not vanish merely because the current F(t) is small. Discarding that old evidence
requires an inference argument. Similarly, small pointwise kernel error does not
bound accumulated score, as the first counterexample shows.

A useful fixed-memory design therefore needs stated approximation obligations:
joint score/precision/query error on an admitted physical set, a valid rule for
evidence and parameter evolution, and explicit failure/unresolved behavior if
the obligations cannot be met. Useful approximation may exploit stimulus
redundancy or query-specific accuracy, but cannot silently turn weak directions
into certainty, narrow physical support, or age away a modeling error.

Keep the total M23 goal approximately10KiB for all owned objects and peak live
workspace; no factor-only count establishes it. For the next desktop comparison,
W<=34 (4760 B packed factor) is a proposed fixed comparison ceiling matching the
earlier compact baseline, not a proved optimal allocation or firmware budget.
Evaluate replacements within that ceiling, retain larger references only as
diagnostics, and report infeasibility rather than raising the ceiling per repair.
