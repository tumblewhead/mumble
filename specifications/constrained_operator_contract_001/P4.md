# P4: a structural error floor for the monotone three-jet family

This is an exact-arithmetic lower bound within P1's repeated-pole family, not
an impossibility for all W34 summaries or all positive realizations. It was
derived while imposing the physical constraints, before any new coefficient
search or fit. Use P1 notation Fhat(d)=exp(-d)(1+alpha*d+beta*d^2), require
khat>=0 for every d>=0, and retain original query t=-1 so exact F(d)=exp(-e*d).
Here e is Euler's number, not the residual defect called e in P2.

## Impulse positivity constrains the coefficients to an ellipse or a cone

P1 gives beta>=0, alpha<=1, and either alpha-2*beta>=0 or

    (alpha-2*beta)^2 <= 4*(1-alpha)*beta.

Expanding the latter is exactly

    alpha^2+(2*beta-1)^2 <= 1.                       (E)

In the first case alpha>=2*beta>=0, so 1+alpha*d+beta*d^2>=1 for d>=0.
In the second case set v=2*beta-1. Cauchy-Schwarz applied to the unit disk (E)
gives, at every d>=0,

    1+alpha*d+beta*d^2
      = 1+d^2/2 + alpha*d + v*d^2/2
      >= 1+d^2/2 - d*sqrt(1+d^2/4).                 (L)

The lower bound is <=1, so it also holds in the first case. At d=1 it becomes

    Fhat(1) >= exp(-1)*(3-sqrt(5))/2.

Consequently at the admitted fast query t=-1,

    Fhat(1)-F(1) >= exp(-1)*(3-sqrt(5))/2 - exp(-e) > 0.    (B)

The displayed expression is about0.0745294 (the checker reports the actual
floating evaluation). The inequality is for every choice of alpha,beta meeting
monotonicity, even without central jet matching or dissipativity. Imposing those
additional conditions cannot weaken the lower bound. This bound is not asserted
to be attainable with all P1 constraints simultaneously or across all t.

For a rational positivity certificate, sqrt(5)<9/4 implies
(3-sqrt(5))/2>3/8. From 8/3<e<3, e-1>5/3 and
exp(e-1)>1+5/3+(5/3)^2/2=73/18>4. Thus exp(-e)<exp(-1)/4,
and (B)>exp(-1)/8>1/24. The standard bounds on e follow from its positive
factorial series (lower through1/3!, upper using n!>=2^(n-1) for n>=2 with
strict inequality later). This gives a fully analytic positive floor independent
of floating evaluation.

There is also an initial-slope obstruction. In the cone alpha>=0. In the ellipse
alpha>=-1. Hence khat(0)/a=1-alpha<=2 throughout the admissible family, while
at t=-1 the exact k(0)/a=e>2. This slope observation alone would not prove a
uniform value-error floor; (L) and (B) supply that missing argument.

## Consequences and limits

A new query-coordinate map over the same stationary centre jets cannot deliver
an arbitrarily accurate monotone free response on the full original support.
For a single isolated rest branch with fixed incoming voltage b, its voltage
response discrepancy at elapsed age tau_c is at least |b| times (B).
It is a fixed-parameter response comparison. Refitting b/tau, cancellation between
branches, small b, and application tolerance change the inference consequences;
it is NOT a lower bound on total terminal-voltage error, Q/SOC error, fitted
forecast error or physical truth error for the battery campaign.

This closes the proposed search for a uniformly small free-kernel error by
query-map tuning alone below this floor. It does not declare all practical use of
the family infeasible. In particular, no application budget has been allocated
that would turn0.0745294 into a universal deployment cutoff. P2's joint criteria
remain necessary. Rest success from an unphysical map cannot waive P1 silently.

The design response is to stop another map-only tuning loop, retain this family
as a reference, and investigate a DIFFERENT fixed-rank realization after its
closure/resource contract. Three distinct fixed poles, or another bounded-state
realization, are hypotheses rather than accepted replacements. P1's positive-bank
obstruction warns that nonnegative weights may force an explicit relaxation of
centre exactness; a signed combination requires its own positivity/energy proof.
No added history features, narrowed physical support, new forgetting law or
promotion is justified. The P3 memory experiment is independent of this choice.
