# P2: selection by joint error and application consequences

This is a design contract and conditional bound, not an accepted error allowance.
Use the original public support, priors, centres, observation identities/order,
noise model and query tasks. Truth is excluded from selection, centring and fitting.
For each declared common physical anchor theta and history h, let r be direct
normalized residual, e the candidate-minus-direct residual, J=Dr,E=De. Include
all state/parameter derivatives and unchanged prior precision Lambda in
P=Lambda+J^T J>0. Define

    Delta g=J^T e+E^T r+E^T e,
    A=P^-1/2 (J^T E+E^T J+E^T E) P^-1/2,
    b=P^-1/2 Delta g.

P1's physical constraints are hard admission conditions. Error selection keeps
these joint objects, never a sum of independent per-parameter error scores.
RC and H defects, including their cross products, stay together. For vector query
q (including state transport), let G=Dq, delta_q=qhat-q,
T=G P^-1/2 and D=(Ghat-G)P^-1/2. Retain both direct and compressed transport.

## Conditional local uncertainty and fitted-query bounds

For affine residual models with the same quadratic prior, assume both the direct
fit theta* and candidate fit thetahat are interior stationary points in the
original support (hence unconstrained minimizers of their positive-definite
quadratics). If ||A||<=epsilon<1, J1 gives exactly

    ||P^1/2(thetahat-theta*)|| <= ||b||/(1-epsilon).

For an affine scalar query qhat with physical-unit tolerance a_q>0, its fitted
error relative to the direct fit is at most

    |delta_q(theta*)| + ||Ghat P^-1/2|| * ||b||/(1-epsilon).       (1)

This follows by substituting the displacement and Cauchy-Schwarz. It does not
bound truth error or cover a constrained nonstationary candidate fit. Original
support need not be convex, so no constrained-fit bound is inferred here. For vector queries, use an explicitly declared diagonal unit/
tolerance scaling Lq, replace absolute value by Euclidean norm and the row norm
by the spectral norm of Lq*Ghat*P^-1/2. Do not invent a conversion between volts,
SOC fractions and ampere-seconds by unscaled addition.

At a fixed common anchor the full local GN query covariance is exactly

    Cq=T T^T, Cqhat=(T+D)(I+A)^-1(T+D)^T.

Let eA=||A||<1. The inverse spectral bounds and expanding (T+D)(T+D)^T prove

    ||Cqhat-Cq|| <= [eA*||T||^2+2||T||*||D||+||D||^2]/(1-eA). (2)

Apply the same Lq scaling to T,D when physical units differ. This is a local
GN covariance statement, not posterior mass, calibrated coverage, or a nonlinear
fit theorem. A failed sufficient bound is inconclusive; report actual generalized
eigenvalues and readouts separately. Unresolved direct fits withhold fitted-query
claims even if common-anchor algebra is numerically defined.

For nonlinear fits, an alternative conditional theorem avoids calling GN exact:
fix P0>0 and a convex region containing theta*,thetahat and their segment; assume
both gradients vanish at their respective fits, and the candidate's EXACT Hessian
satisfies Hhat(theta)>=m P0 with m>0 throughout the region. Then

    ||P0^1/2(thetahat-theta*)|| <= ||P0^-1/2 Delta g(theta*)||/m.

Proof: integrate the candidate Hessian along the segment to form Hbar>=m P0.
The gradient difference equation is Hbar*(thetahat-theta*)=-Delta g(theta*).
Whiten and invert. If sup_segment ||Dqhat P0^-1/2||<=L, fitted scalar query
error is at most |delta_q(theta*)|+L*||P0^-1/2 Delta g||/m by integration.
Without these premises, use measured refits with explicit numerical status;
never substitute positive GN curvature for this Hessian certificate.

## Selection protocol; no scalar trade that hides a failure

Freeze W<=34 and factor<=4760B; freeze the same 312B response state for this
next subfamily. Preserve original support and physical parameter continuity.
Require P1 impulse and energy inequalities over ALL t in [-1,1], central jet
conditions for this subfamily, chronology, one evidence owner, full joint root,
and a finite implementable coefficient/derivative program. A sampled pass is
not a proof over support. A candidate failing a hard constraint is rejected;
a promising but uncertified one remains unresolved. No silent clipping of its
response, feature derivative, support, or posterior is allowed.

For admitted candidates report the vector over registered histories/anchors:

    (max ||b||, max ||A||, scaled same-anchor query error,
     scaled fitted-query error/bound, query covariance discrepancy,
     certified response/sensitivity remainder, peak SRAM, compute).

Use a Pareto comparison first. If a minimax selection is later used, register
all scaling budgets before fitting. epsilon<1 has a mathematical invertibility
meaning, not the status of a practical tolerance. The existing approximate1%
Q and0.5% SOC objectives are application guidance, not newly allocated compressor
budgets; SOC fraction vs percentage points must be explicit in a registration.
Brownout voltage margin and load horizon remain owner-dependent. No global
accept/reject number is invented in this stage. Local tiny SDs may not stand in
for physical error tolerances, yet genuine covariance distortion stays visible.

Registered evidence families must include rest, weak/repetitive, signed pulses,
embedded switching, off-centre original-support cases, different noise levels,
and longer prefixes. Keep the previous six cases as regression evidence, not
independent confirmation after using them for design. Construct development and
held-out seeds/streams before any optimization; never tune on held-out outcomes.
No candidate optimization or new fit is performed in this contract stage.

## Sustained use: a necessary unresolved requirement

For N prefixes, bounded per-record response defect does not ensure bounded b
or fitted error relative to shrinking local uncertainty (B1's coherent-bias
counterexample). All-time feasibility would require the stated bounds uniformly
over admitted histories AND prefixes, plus rounding and an evolution model.
Do not infer this from a finite set of trajectory checks. A deterministic response
error envelope must propagate as approximation uncertainty or set-valued query
error; it is not fresh independent noise per record. Merely adding a variance
floor or a forgetting factor lacks a derivation here.

This contract selects a stationary approximation only. Monotone health/thermal
laws and their joint uncertainty need their own boundary-message closure and
error propagation. Improved physical constraints cannot repair H approximation,
aging or lifetime authorization by themselves. If the fixed family cannot meet
these obligations, record infeasibility of that family and revisit the allocation
or architecture explicitly; do not grow rank one parameter at a time.
