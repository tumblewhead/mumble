# M1: physical partition attribution in one joint uncertainty metric

Premises: stationary linear-OCV Foster/hysteresis reference; same ordered data,
known sigma>0, original prior x=mean+Lw with nonsingular L; same admitted interior
anchor x; independent physical coordinates c=(a,log(s)), with eleven amplitudes
and five rates as defined by conditional_packet.partition. Let B=dx/dc, nonsingular
in the interior, and K=B^-1 L. Derivatives below are whitened by sigma. Both exact
and approximate responses are additive in their physical partitions. No noisy
measurement is selected or discarded and no old posterior is added as new evidence.

## Partition before changing coordinates

RC voltage depends on c columns R={1,2,3,6,7,8,11,12,13}. H voltage depends on
H={9,10,14,15}. OCV/charge/ohmic terms depend on O={0,4,5}. Indices are zero-based;
H rates are gamma_plus/Q and gamma_minus/Q, not the solver's separate gammas.
The partition is disjoint in c, not necessarily after transformation back to x or w.

If J_c and Jhat_c are the direct and compressed voltage Jacobians, set D_Rc to
their difference in R columns and zero elsewhere, and D_Hc analogously. In exact
arithmetic the unchanged O columns have zero difference. Thus

    J_w=J_c K, D_R=D_Rc K, D_H=D_Hc K,
    Jhat_w=J_w+D_R+D_H.

These are ordinary chain-rule identities. In particular H defects can affect Q
through K. Masking solver columns instead would generally implement a different
decomposition. A nonzero numerical O-column residual must be reported and checked
against the full compressed control; it cannot silently become an H or RC effect.

Because the model is linear in amplitudes at fixed rates, normalized residual
defects are e_R=sum_(i in R intersect [0,10]) D_Rc[:,i] a_i, and analogously e_H.
This gives optional cost/gradient attribution without separate evidence ownership.

## Keep interactions and prior once

Put P=I+J_w^T J_w and P_S=I+(J_w+D_S)^T(J_w+D_S), with
D_S=D_R, D_H, or D_R+D_H. Then

    Delta P_combined=Delta P_R+Delta P_H+D_R^T D_H+D_H^T D_R.

Expansion proves the identity. The interaction is generally nonzero even though
R/H have disjoint columns in c. Norms of these pieces are not variance fractions.
All variants use the same single identity prior in w coordinates.

A common orthonormal reduction of the augmented row block
[J_w,D_Rc,D_Hc,r,e_R,e_H] preserves every inner product used here. Thin QR may
therefore reduce that whole block incrementally. It must be one common reduction,
not independent Q factors for separate terms. Padding with zero rows is harmless.
Actual observations are still owned once. Floating QR and subsequent solves are
checked numerically; the identity itself is exact arithmetic.

## Whitened metric and queries

For P=C C^T, C lower triangular, let M=C^-T. Then M^T P M=I.
P3 can use J_w M and D_S M instead of its symmetric P^-1/2: M=P^-1/2 O for
an orthogonal O, so singular values and relative precision eigenvalues agree.
Define eta_S=||D_S M||_2, epsilon_S=2 eta_S+eta_S^2 and

    W_S=(J_w M)^T(D_S M)+(D_S M)^T(J_w M)+(D_S M)^T(D_S M).

The exact-arithmetic spectrum of P_S relative to P is that of I+W_S. If
epsilon_S<1, P3's inverse and forecast bounds apply. If not, that sufficient test
is inconclusive. A measured positive minimum eigenvalue may still show that a
particular local matrix is nonsingular; it is not a global or interval certificate.

For a row query gradient g_w and variant ghat_w, q=||g_w M|| and
h=||(ghat_w-g_w)M||. P3 bounds the local variant variance by
max(0,q-h)^2/(1+epsilon_S) and (q+h)^2/(1-epsilon_S). Using direct g_w for all
variants isolates precision. Using each hybrid's transported gradient includes
transport effects. Forecast gradients must include the incoming-state Jacobian.
RC-only hybrid transport replaces branch-voltage coordinates and their Jacobian
rows; H-only replaces the transformed hysteresis coordinate/row; combined replaces
both. Static parameters and SOC transport remain the same. Require the resulting
physical chart and future trajectory to be supported; otherwise report unsupported.
These are same-anchor counterfactual models, not fitted replacements or coverage.

## P2's universal RC envelope in this metric

At a fixed anchor, let b_c contain P3a's absolute partial-derivative bounds in R
columns and zeros elsewhere. With fixed Imax over the prefix, each whitened row
obeys |D_Rc[k,:]|<=b_c/sigma_k. Consequently

    ||D_R M||_2 <= ||D_R M||_F
       <= sqrt(sum_k sigma_k^-2) * ||b_c |K M|||_2.

The last inequality uses entrywise absolute matrix multiplication and the triangle
inequality on each row. This can be much looser than the measured defect and can
grow with observation count even though b_c is independent of elapsed duration.
It applies to Taylor2 only; there is no new universal H bound in this study.
The known precision metric is an input to this inequality, not supplied by P2.
No finite numerical eigenvalue, small point error or completed local fit establishes
valid physical coverage, indefinite history fidelity or changing-parameter behavior.
