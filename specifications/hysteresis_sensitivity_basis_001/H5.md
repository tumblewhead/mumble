# H5: six chronological hysteresis sensitivities

Assume H1's stationary positive rates k_plus=gamma_plus/Q and k_minus=gamma_minus/Q,
fixed reference temperature, known ordered current/intervals and initial h0
independent of the rates. Let t_j=log(k_j/k_cj) about the original public centres.
Use h=F h0+u with F=1,u=0 initially. B=H h0 and H are separate amplitudes, so the
hysteresis voltage is B F+H u. All identities here are exact arithmetic.

## Differentiate the physical recurrence

For sign s and throughput U=|I|dt/3600, active direction j, let d=k_j U,
E=exp(-d), e1=-d E and e2=(d^2-d)E. Then Fnew=E F and unew=E u+(1-E)s.
These log-rate derivative signs differ from log-tau derivatives of the RC decay.
With other direction l, product-rule differentiation gives, for f=F or u,

    fnew = E f + (1-E)c,
    f_j,new = E f_j + e1 (f-c),
    f_l,new = E f_l,
    f_jj,new = E f_jj + 2 e1 f_j + e2 (f-c),
    f_jl,new = E f_jl + e1 f_l,
    f_ll,new = E f_ll,

where c=0 for F and c=s for u. All right-hand values are pre-interval values.
The mixed derivative of E is zero because one interval's E depends on only its
active rate. The carried f_jl still contains all preceding reversal effects; it
is not dropped. Initially every derivative is zero. At rest no state changes.
Induction over ordered intervals proves the six states equal the corresponding
response derivatives at the fixed centre. They are features, not new observations.

Free response has the independent check F=exp(-D_plus-D_minus), D_j=k_j U_j:
F_j=-D_j F, F_jj=(D_j^2-D_j)F, F_plus,minus=D_plus D_minus F.
For a single sign throughout, u=s(1-F), and all nonzero-order u derivatives are
-s times the free derivatives. Under reversal, equal total directional throughput
does not determine u; these recurrence states preserve the actual chronology.

## Six coefficients, same factor width

For either response define the degree-two Taylor polynomial

    P(t)=f00+f10 t_plus+f01 t_minus
         +f20 t_plus^2/2+f11 t_plus t_minus+f02 t_minus^2/2.

It matches value, gradient and Hessian at t=0. In the existing Chebyshev pair
order [(0,0),(0,1),(0,2),(1,0),(1,1),(2,0)] its coefficients are

    [f00+(f20+f02)/4, f01, f02/4, f10, f11, f20/4].

The mixed coefficient has no extra factor of two: the symmetric Hessian Taylor
term already combines its two off-diagonal terms. The coefficient map follows
from t^2=(T2(t)+1)/2. Apply it independently to F and u. The existing linear H/B
amplitude lifting then gives exact central amplitude/rate partials, followed by
the unchanged full chain rule into the solver chart, including Q in gamma/Q.

The joint factor retains its six H coefficients per amplitude and every cross
term with RC, OCV and charge integration. One ordered voltage sample supplies one
row. Incoming/outgoing state transport uses these same response polynomials and
their derivatives. No support restriction, recentering or evidence discount follows.

The Taylor-RC/Taylor-H field still has 34 lifted entries and 4760 B packed factor.
This prototype pads the six H states to nine slots per free/forced response to use
the existing transport layout: response state 312 B, domain/scalars 96 B, lower
subtotal 5168 B. Unused slots remain zero. Removing padding is a separate possible
48 B optimization, not part of this result. Full target memory/time is unqualified.
