# Compact hysteresis value-and-sensitivity study

Base 05c67e0, branch experiment/conditional-architecture-001. Keep fixed public
centres/support and the existing continuous model, prior and observation ownership.
No refit, production change or long campaign is authorized by these diagnostics.

H5 derives six streamed derivatives of free/forced hysteresis response: value,
both first derivatives, both pure second derivatives and their mixed derivative.
They form a quadratic Taylor polynomial in the original log(gamma/Q) coordinates.
H6 supplies a conservative duration-independent third-derivative envelope and
local remainder bounds. These are stationary exact-input/exact-arithmetic claims,
not calibrated uncertainty, numerical lifetime or aging proofs.

Compare at all twelve saved direct/Taylor anchors from the preceding pilot/full
study (rest, multiscale and embedded). All principal arms use the SAME Taylor RC:
H2, c22-only completion, full quadratic tensor, Taylor H2 and H7. Retain the old
R5_H7 reference separately. The new native kernel supports derivative H and matched
collocation H without changing RC arithmetic. Its distinct symbol/mode contract
must prevent loading it through a legacy row interface.

Repeat the physical partition/common QR diagnostics of M1: RC-only, H-only and
combined eta/epsilon, actual joint precision spectra, Q/SOC widths, cost/gradient
defects, direct versus hybrid transport forecasts, unsupported status and source
identity checks. Compare unchanged H2/R5_H7 controls to prior saved factors and
metrics. Do not let precision/transport compensation count as success.

Separately evaluate a public signed-current/rest fixture at a fixed centre and a
two-dimensional off-centre grid, checking values, both rate derivatives, mixed
derivatives and amplitude dependence. Include rest and one-sign identities, small
and irregular/zero intervals, chunking, full joint QR, failure atomicity and kernel
binding. Numerical identities have arithmetic tolerances, not product thresholds.

Individually review H5/H6 and review native/Python implementation and results.
Preserve failures and re-review substantive repairs. A central-matching candidate
may fail away from centre; disclose failures without shrinking the support.

Taylor H retains six response coefficients and a 4760 B factor. This prototype
pads six derivative slots to the existing nine-slot H layout for shared transport:
live response stays 312 B, partial factor/response/domain subtotal 5168 B. A later
six-slot layout could save 48 B but is not implemented or credited here. c22 and
full-tensor factors are 5328 and 6560 B; Taylor-RC/H7 factor is 35720 B. These
exclude prior, solver, tables, scratch, metadata and platform overhead.
