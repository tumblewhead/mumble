# A5 — Joint query computation, trust and useful availability

## Query interface and semantic classes

A query names quantity/units, model/configuration/epoch, actual physical prefix,
validity horizon, tolerances and requested guarantee kind. SOC, reference or
temperature-adjusted Q, and a minimum terminal voltage under a proposed load
are different queries. A proposed-load query includes the future I/T/duration
envelope, voltage floor/margin and uncertainty about future disturbances/aging.

Return point/range, source ownership, solver/representation/transport status,
guarantee kind and authorization separately. A finite point can be useful while
its uncertainty is provisional. Local GN precision is not posterior mass or
physical coverage. A wide outer set means authorization is unresolved; it does
not establish an impossible battery or an intrinsically unsafe action. Every
nuisance direction affecting a query must remain in the joint calculation even
when its marginal estimate is not useful.

## Exact local projection theorem

Let P=R^T R be SPD in specified common tangent coordinates, with nonsingular
upper-triangular R. Let j be the row derivative of the requested scalar in those
same coordinates, including all state/parameter transport. Solve R^T u=j^T.
Then j P^-1 j^T=j R^-1 R^-T j^T=u^T u. This follows by inversion of R^T R and
substitution. For two queries, covariance is u_1^T u_2. No diagonalization or
independence assumption is used, and no explicit full inverse is needed.

This theorem concerns a local quadratic model only. If P is singular, a
pseudoinverse must not silently turn an unresolved null direction into zero
variance. Use a separately justified support/identifiability treatment or return
unresolved. A diagonal original prior does not make the posterior diagonal.
Transforming coordinates requires the original prior/support/measure and all
query derivatives to follow the map. Profiling amplitudes is not marginalization;
K7's determinant and bounded-support mass terms must be retained where applicable.

For a computed uhat, let the exact residual be r=R^T uhat-j^T. If a verified
bound ||R^-T||<=M and ||r||<=rho holds, then ||uhat-u||<=M*rho by the inverse
relation and submultiplicativity. Hence ||u|| lies between
max(0,||uhat||-M*rho) and ||uhat||+M*rho. Squaring gives variance bounds. Norms,
residuals and M must be outward enclosed, and error in R/j relative to the
intended local model is additional A6 error. An estimated condition number or
residual alone is not a certificate of this premise.

## Authorization theorem and proof

Assume a nonempty joint admissible set C contains the true state at every relevant
decision on one qualified simultaneous event E, P(E)>=1-alpha. A verified query
enclosure [l,u] includes f(C), numerical/transport defects and the requested
validity horizon. For output q authorize absolute tolerance t only when
max(|q-l|,|q-u|)<=t. Convexity of absolute distance proves |q-f(true)|<=t on E.
For positive capacity support and relative tolerance0<=t<1, authorize only if
(1-t)u<=q<=(1+t)l; intersecting (1-t)Q<=q<=(1+t)Q over Q in[l,u] proves it.
Thus any unsafe authorization lies in E's complement (reviewed D2).

For load admission, a verified lower bound must exceed the declared voltage
floor plus margin throughout the entire future load horizon, including edges
and extrema. Current-state coverage alone cannot bound unknown future inputs.
Repeated pointwise Gaussian intervals do not establish E; D3 or another valid
simultaneous-risk construction must supply it. Conditional model guarantees must
name their physical/input/noise assumptions, not hide them behind a trust bit.

## Availability, failure and acceptance

Specify an achievable stimulus/power/sensor scenario class and a useful-query
deadline. Under one scenario law, if valid containment fails with probability at
most alpha and qualifying query production/publication by the deadline fails with
probability at most beta, the union bound gives correct-and-available probability
at least max(0,1-alpha-beta), as in D4. Same-law and deadline premises matter after
adaptive scenario/fault selection. Always refusing is not a useful availability
result. D0 prevents demanding perfect trust classification for indistinguishable
good/bad situations; unresolved does not assert an estimate is inaccurate.

Required witnesses: strongly correlated and weak/null directions; direct full-root
versus scalar solve; false marginal-independence counterexamples; near-singular
residual bounds; stale prefix/horizon; physical/support/model failures; no-stimulus
controls and achievable informative recovery. Validate forecast minima over the
whole action, not only sampled endpoints. Coverage and availability populations,
lifetime alpha/beta semantics, tolerance allocation and deadlines remain explicit
requirements to qualify. No new query implementation or calibrated interval is
claimed by this contract.
