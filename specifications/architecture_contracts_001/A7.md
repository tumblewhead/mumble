# A7 — Aging, temperature and physical state transport

## Physical evolution interface

Separate reversible thermal response from reference-condition health. The
proposed aging directions are nondecreasing reference resistances and
nonincreasing reference Q and branch C_i=tau_i/R_i. These are hypotheses to
qualify for the battery/use family, not universal electrochemistry. Tau=R*C has
no implied monotonic direction. Pole ordering/branch identity under possible
crossings needs a qualified relabeling/domain policy preserving the same physical
voltage; sorting scalar estimates without joint-state transport is not that policy.

An evolution relation F_k maps joint physical/parameter state and qualified
inputs/elapsed interval to possible successor states. Alternatively a probabilistic
transition law specifies the same variables, support, normalization, uncertainty
and correlations. Select and validate the semantics; do not interchange a
set envelope and a probability density. Drift may depend on elapsed time,
temperature and throughput only when that dependence is qualified. A monotone
spline or codebook approximates an admitted trajectory family with an explicit
remainder; knot count alone is not evidence that real aging belongs to it.

The proposed0.5–1.5 range (more likely30%) is an engineering envelope awaiting
qualification. Nominal60mAh manufacturing guidance +/-3sigma=2.6–3.6mAh supplies
neither a distribution shape nor an aging rate. Unknown current/temperature/clock
and thermal coefficients enter the evolution/input uncertainty explicitly.

## Conditional containment theorem

Assume x_0 in C_0 and actual (x_k,u_k) lie in the qualified joint input/state
relation. Let R_k contain all successors admitted by F_k from C_k and those
inputs. Let Y_(k+1) contain the true successor under qualified measurement
semantics. Store an outer set C_(k+1) containing R_k intersect Y_(k+1), including
rounded/compressed computation. Then the true successor belongs to both R_k and
Y_(k+1), hence C_(k+1). Induction proves containment at every finite prefix
(reviewed D1). A probabilistic simultaneous statement requires these premises
to hold on one qualified event; it is not obtained by repeatedly declaring an
independent per-step confidence level.

With a valid monotone increase bound0<=p_(k+1)-p_k<=d_k, interval[l,u] propagates
to[l,u+d_k]; with a decrease bound it propagates to[l-d_k,u]. The inequalities
prove these inclusions directly. Monotonicity without a quantitative rate or
other qualified temporal restriction permits traversing the entire remaining
support in an arbitrarily short positive interval (D5). It cannot justify a
tighter drift limit than that remaining support. Near an endpoint, the remaining
support can itself be narrow, so useful narrowness is not categorically impossible
without a rate bound. A stationary special case uses d_k=0 only where
stationarity itself is qualified, not because the estimator lacks an aging model.

## Historical evidence and state consistency

Old observations constrain old parameters. Their effect on the present travels
through the qualified joint path/evolution relation. Charge integration uses the
capacity at each interval. Under the declared integrator dz/dt=-I/(3600Q) with
piecewise-constant effective capacity and SOC continuity at capacity boundaries,
z_N=z_0-sum I_k*dt_k/(3600Q_k). This is a conditional state convention: a law
preserving absolute charge while Q changes, or a different loss mechanism, may
require boundary jumps or additional terms. The selected F_k must explicitly
include that transport; the sum is not asserted for every possible aging law.
Even under the SOC-continuous convention, equal final Q and total signed charge do
not determine that sum when Q varied. A current health centroid therefore cannot
replace the joint accumulated path and its uncertainty without a bounded argument.

For changing RC capacitance, preserving branch voltage and preserving branch
charge C*v yield different transition relations. The physical family must select
or bound the valid relation. Hysteresis evolution, temperature uncertainty, sensor
offset drift and parameter correlations likewise need explicit state/law treatment.
Do not add an old posterior and its observations as separate evidence. If using
probabilistic marginalization, preserve state-dependent normalizers, support masses
and coordinate Jacobians; bounded transitions need not close under composition.

## Failure, recovery and acceptance

If observed behavior leaves the qualified evolution family, diagnose model-support
failure and suspend affected authorization; do not silently expand a bound after
seeing truth or relabel temperature changes as irreversible aging. With lost
history, initialize from an aged-cell-valid public support and declare unresolved
directions. Any useful reacquisition deadline needs an achievable stimulus and
power scenario under A5/A8. Physical relaxation alone does not erase statistical
evidence or its approximation error.

Acceptance needs independently characterized aging/thermal/input envelopes or
probabilistic laws, path-order/equal-final-health counterexamples, state-map checks,
quiet periods, temperature cycles, sensor drift, pole interactions and reset of an
aged cell. Measure both false authorization and useful availability. No numerical
drift rates, knot count, process noise, forgetting factor or deployable aging model
is chosen or proved by this contract.
