# A8 — Complete resources, scheduling and system behavior

## Resource and service interface

The target is a baseline M23 in custom-silicon Verilog simulation at16MHz with
roughly10KiB SRAM, nominal35Hz configurable acquisition and a ten-year design
horizon. These are engineering targets, not measured bounds. Declare the actual
clock/service guarantee, maximum admitted F_s, arrival jitter, interrupt/blocking
model, input/output deadlines and allowed backlog before implementation acceptance.

Account for all persistent state, likelihood/shape representation, prior/support,
cache/registry keys, transaction backup, pending inputs, publication/checkpoint
buffers, allocator/alignment overhead, stack and worst feasible ISR nesting.
Code/lookup tables and soft-float/libm/ABI support need a separate linked-image
budget. Persistent working history remains SRAM-only; no external flash/FRAM
history is assumed as an escape from the memory target. Reset must use A4/A7's
qualified loss-of-history path unless a separately authorized mechanism exists.

## Conditional memory and work theorem

Suppose a verified concrete layout reserves disjoint physical regions of sizes
M_static,M_arena,M_stack,M_other. Assume every admitted allocation/alias fits its
region under A3, stack/interrupt nesting fits M_stack, and all other memory belongs
to M_other. Summing disjoint region sizes bounds total SRAM by their sum (K5).
Payload byte counts, individual stack frames and observed RSS cannot establish
these containment premises. Include metadata/version-counter capacity for the
full horizon and qualified reset/migration behavior before overflow.

Partition actual work into disjoint setup/sample/block/query/refresh/recovery/
other categories. If category j has at most n_j executions costing at most C_j
cycles each, total cost<=sum n_j*C_j by summing the bounds. Count retries, cache
misses, failed searches, certificates, restores and diagnostics in these categories.
No expected cache hit rate or typical iteration count substitutes for worst-path
bounds. A bounded solver cap gives termination, not a useful estimate.

For a FIFO ready job of cost<=C with at most W cycles ahead, assume useful service
over every backlogged interval of duration t is at least s*max(0,t-J), s>0. If
W+C<=s*max(0,D-J), the job completes within D: otherwise the queue stayed
backlogged and by D would have served all earlier work and this job, a contradiction.
This is K5's service premise; nominal16MHz alone does not supply it. Sample/block
collection latency, output freshness and sensor jitter are additional, measured
on the same time basis. A bound on W requires an arrival/backlog argument.

## Overload, fault and lifetime behavior

On overload, refuse work or enter a declared degraded/quarantined mode before
violating evidence ownership or serving stale authority. The resulting missing
physical interval requires A4/A7 gap handling. Do not silently drop samples to
meet CPU targets, use an unbounded queue, or repeatedly retry beyond the declared
budget. Worst-case availability and fallback power safety belong to A5; resource
boundedness alone does not make permanent refusal acceptable.

Specify whether reset/power loss/interrupt faults are admitted and the service
and storage consequences. End-of-horizon overflow must lead to qualified rollover
with invalidation or controlled refusal, not reused generations. Ten years bounds
resource/counter obligations; it does not authorize statistical risk to restart
per boot or per optimizer invocation.

## Cadence and closed-loop theorem

In a deterministic controller/environment with identical initial states and a
common exogenous disturbance sequence, assume the two estimator versions emit
identical public values, status and relevant timing for every common input prefix.
Induct over external events: identical outputs give identical control actions;
the environment then gives identical next observations and states. This preserves
the common-prefix premise (K6). Stochastic comparison needs an appropriate common
coupling. Changed latency, approximate outputs or action-triggered sampling violate
this premise unless separately bounded.

For bounded changes, qualify decision margins at every reachable joint state or
a joint estimator/controller/plant reachability relation. Close replay estimates
on one saved input do not prove the same realized future stimulus. Multiple safe
actions may be acceptable, but their excitation/availability is a new policy
claim, not identical behavior.

## Acceptance evidence and open quantities

Require complete linker/map/layout records, stack and interrupt call-chain bounds,
target worst-path cycle accounting, cold cache/miss/overflow/recovery cases,
backlog/admission/freshness proofs and timed controller/load tests. Measure the
actual16MHz target implementation; host response speedups are only design evidence.
The schedule, maximum rate, reserve budget, blackout interval, deadline, risk of
unavailable service and fallback action remain requirements to bind, not invented
constants. No final layout, executive, target cycle bound or deployable system is
established by this conditional resource/composition contract.
