# P4: independent free/forced bases in one joint likelihood

Premises: fixed positive reference R,tau and known thermal multipliers independent
of those learned amplitudes/shapes, finite strictly positive thermal time
multipliers and observation sigma, ordered finite observations with nonnegative
actual intervals, finite initial branch voltage b and piecewise constant inputs.
All algebra below is exact arithmetic. Numerical rounding, unknown thermal laws,
aging and nonlinear inference/coverage require separate qualifications.

For interval k write d_k=dt_k/(tau*f_tau,k), e_k=exp(-d_k),
j_k=f_R,k I_k. The exact update v_new=e_k v+(1-e_k)R j_k gives

    v_k=b F_k+R u_k,
    F_new=e_k F,                  F_0=1,
    u_new=e_k u+(1-e_k)j_k,       u_0=0.

Substitution and induction prove the decomposition, including zero intervals.
If every I_k=0 then every u_k=0 for every tau. Thus all tau derivatives of u_k
also vanish. The retained incoming voltage is b, not u_0; it is never reset at a
later rest. A later rest following active use generally has u != 0 and does not
satisfy this zero-origin restriction. No rig knowledge is required by the updates.

Use fixed t=log(tau/tau_c) in the original [-1,1] box. Approximate independently

    Fhat(t)=sum_(i=0..p) f_i T_i(t),
    uhat(t)=sum_(j=0..q) g_j T_j(t).

The voltage contribution remains linear in features b*T_i(t) and R*T_j(t).
For each observation, concatenate these features for every RC branch with the
existing OCV, charge and hysteresis features and the constant residual column.
One row dot product equals the approximate voltage residual divided by sigma.
One QR factor of these rows preserves its complete quadratic likelihood, including
all cross-family terms. Different basis degrees do not create independent evidence
or permit separate covariance blocks. Append the original prior once at fitting.

For free response use p=3 or5 at the existing public Chebyshev nodes
t_l=cos(pi*(l+1/2)/(p+1)); stream F at those nodes and use the fixed inverse
Vandermonde to obtain f. For forced response retain q=2 with exact streamed
u,u_t,u_tt at t=0 from P1; its Chebyshev coefficients are
[u+u_tt/4,u_t,u_tt/4]. The free nodes and Taylor centre are public and never move.
At zero-origin current this construction gives g=0 exactly, and the approximate
likelihood is independent of which forced basis was stored. With p=5 it agrees
algebraically with full degree-five RC on that restriction, provided the same H
response is used. With zero current the Taylor-H and H7 functions themselves
coincide in exact arithmetic, so the R5/H7 objective also agrees. QR and fit
trajectories may still differ by floating rounding; the theorem does not promise
bitwise equal nonlinear fits or numerical convergence.

Define response defects dF=Fhat-F and du=uhat-u. For fixed b,R,

    dv=b*dF+R*du,
    partial_t dv=b*dF_t+R*du_t.

Amplitude derivatives of dv are dF and du respectively. These identities prove
why zero-current defects isolate free response, but not that the larger free
basis improves active histories or every point of the support. Initial voltages
and forced responses can compensate in a fit; evaluate both likelihood and
endpoint transport, retaining full coordinate chains and uncertainty. For valid
pointwise bounds EF,EU,DF,DU the triangle inequality gives
|dv| <= |b|EF+R EU and |partial_t dv| <= |b|DF+R DU. No new uniform collocation
derivative bound, global likelihood certificate or lifetime error bound is asserted.

The lifted width with three branches, six H terms in each of two families and
the existing four scalar features is W=16+3(p+1)+3(q+1). Packed double triangular
storage is 4W(W+1) bytes: p/q=2/2 gives4760, 3/2 gives5624, 5/2 gives7568,
5/5 gives11024. This is factor storage only. A padded implementation may use more
response state and scratch; all such allocations must be reported independently.
