# A0: dependency structure of the conditional likelihood

Status: conditional proof submitted for individual review. No compression scheme
or continuous-rate estimator is certified by this statement.

Assume the stationary, exact-input, linear-OCV, fixed reference-temperature family
of reviewed R0. The five shapes are s=(tau1,tau2,tau3,kplus,kminus), kdir=gamma/Q.
The eleven amplitudes and the augmented constant have the same meaning as R0.
Measurement standard deviations are positive fixed observed/public weights,
independent of amplitudes and shapes. No model-dependent noise weights are added.

## Dependency lemma

Let the whitened augmented voltage row be r(s) with12entries, and let G(s) be
the sum of r(s)r(s)^T over a finite ordered prefix. Partition the columns:

- U=(z0,q,R0,constant),4columns with no shape dependencies;
- C_i=(v_i0,R_i),2columns depending only on tau_i, for i=1,2,3;
- H=(H,B),2columns depending on the pair(kplus,kminus).

R0 gives these dependencies directly: SOC uses only signed charge; each RC
recurrence uses its own tau; directional hysteresis uses the two directional
rates and chronological input. Induction in the recurrences proves no additional
shape argument enters any listed column. The measured-voltage constant may vary
with time but is independent of the queried shapes.

Each entry G_ij=sum r_i r_j therefore depends on at most the union of its two
column dependency sets. Multiplication and finite addition introduce no new
arguments. Counting the upper triangle including its diagonal gives:

| Groups | Number | Maximum dependency |
|---|---:|---|
|U with U|4*5/2=10|none|
|C_i with U or C_i|3*(2*4+2*3/2)=33|one RC rate|
|C_i with C_j, i<j|3*2*2=12|two RC rates|
|H with U or H|2*4+2*3/2=11|both hysteresis rates|
|C_i with H|3*2*2=12|one RC plus both hysteresis rates|

The sum is78=12*13/2; every entry is counted once. These are upper bounds on
dependencies; a particular stimulus may make some coefficients zero or cancel
dependencies. None requires more than three shapes in this family.

## Consequences and boundaries

All cross terms remain present. This is not a factorization of the posterior
into independent physical partitions. G is positive semidefinite because
x^T G x=sum(r^T x)^2>=0, but independently approximated entries need not preserve
that property. QR factorization, solving, transformed priors and marginalization
can mix the dependency groups. The78entries are functions of continuous shapes,
not78scalar stored constants. The lemma establishes no finite exact function
basis, interpolation error, rounding bound, memory budget or optimizer convergence.

The machine-readable dependency list is checked by independent enumeration of
unordered column pairs. That finite check validates the declared map/count,
not the physical premises or an implementation of every coefficient function.
Known time-varying thermal factors suggest the same dependency map, but are not
silently included in this invocation of R0; aging and uncertain calibration
require their own derivations before extending this contract.
