# U1: conditional representation-bias and joint-uncertainty accounting

Status: symbolic packet for independent review; no new experiment or accepted
numerical allowance. Dependencies: [J1](../symbolic_fixed_budget_001/J1.md) and
[P2](../constrained_operator_contract_001/P2.md). Their full score defect, joint

not establish accuracy, coverage, lifetime operation or Cortex-M23 feasibility.

## 1. A precise linearized bias problem

Fix one physical query point, one ordered prefix of unique observations and its
unchanged prior. All derivatives include physical-state transport. Let Jhat be
the n by p candidate residual Jacobian in physical residual units, W a positive
diagonal observation precision, and H a symmetric positive definite matrix with
H >= Jhat^T W Jhat. A GN matrix with nonnegative prior precision satisfies this;
an exact nonlinear Hessian need not. Set C=H^-1, let c be the column gradient of
a scalar query, and v=Cc. For an additive residual perturbation d, define ONLY
its frozen-J linearized displacement and query bias by

    delta_theta = -C Jhat^T W d,
    b_c = -v^T Jhat^T W d.

This is exact for the corresponding fixed-design quadratic problem with the
same prior. It is a linearization elsewhere, not the complete shift between two
nonlinear fits or between truth and a fitted estimate.

Assume |d_k| <= epsilon_k with epsilon_k >= 0. In a bank decomposition require
nonnegative envelopes e_kl and coefficients gamma_l >= 0 such that

epsilon_k = sum_l gamma_l e_kl. These may be evaluated at the query only when
the required pointwise bound is proved there; a support-uniform statement needs
the bound everywhere on the declared support. Signed bank interpolation weights
do not themselves provide nonnegative error envelopes.

Three simultaneous upper bounds follow. Write W=I/sigma^2 for the last two
formulas and let M_i >= max_k |Jhat_ki| at this same query:

    B_CS  = sqrt(c^T C c) sqrt(sum_k epsilon_k^2 / sigma^2),
    B_L1  = (sum_i |v_i| M_i) (sum_k epsilon_k) / sigma^2,
    K     = sum_k epsilon_k Jhat_k^T Jhat_k,
    B_WCS = sqrt(v^T K v) sqrt(sum_k epsilon_k) / sigma^2.

For general diagonal W, B_CS=sqrt(c^T C c)||epsilon||_W;
the other two replace each summation weight 1/sigma^2 by W_kk (and use
K_W=sum_k W_kk epsilon_k Jhat_k^T Jhat_k, giving
sqrt(v^T K_W v) sqrt(sum_k W_kk epsilon_k)).

Proof of CS: |b_c| <= ||Jhat v||_W ||d||_W, while
v^T Jhat^T W Jhat v <= v^T H v=c^T C c. Proof of L1:
|Jhat_k v| <= sum_i |v_i| M_i and apply the triangle inequality to the
weighted score. Proof of WCS: first bound |b_c| by
sum_k epsilon_k |Jhat_k v| / sigma^2, then apply Cauchy-Schwarz to
sqrt(epsilon_k) and sqrt(epsilon_k)|Jhat_k v|. Zero total envelope gives
zero bias directly. All three retain the full correlated C, including nuisance
coordinates. They do not allocate independent errors to fitted parameters.

Thus min(B_CS,B_L1,B_WCS) is valid. If d=sum_l d_l and
|d_lk| <= gamma_l e_kl, compute the three bounds separately for each d_l
and sum their minima. Each minimum bounds that component's scalar projection;
the triangle inequality proves the sum without any independence assumption.
This sum need not equal or beat the minimum computed for the total envelope;
the minimum of those two valid totals is also valid.

## 2. Statistics and their actual resource obligation

If all e_kl are fixed history features independent of the final parameter query,
let s_l=sum_k e_kl and T_lm=sum_k e_kl e_km. Then
sum epsilon=gamma^T s and sum epsilon^2=gamma^T T gamma. A full symmetric
T requires L(L+1)/2 scalars; s needs L. Each record updates these quantities,
so persistent scalar count is duration independent, but arithmetic error is not.
If the envelopes themselves depend on the eventual query, these moments require
a proved finite feature expansion or regeneration, not mere substitution.

At a fixed query K=sum_l gamma_l K_l with
K_l=sum_k e_kl Jhat_k^T Jhat_k. Retaining these requires L p(p+1)/2
scalars in a packed symmetric representation, plus working storage and the
other statistics. WCS is not a scalar-only ledger. M_i likewise needs a proved
bound valid at the final query; maxima computed at changing online fits are
insufficient.

In particular, accumulating Jhat_k(theta_k)^T Jhat_k(theta_k) online and
later labelling it Jhat(theta_final)^T Jhat(theta_final) is invalid in general.
If Jhat_k(theta)=f_k^T B(theta), with history features f_k in R^m fixed at
acquisition and exact for the represented model, one may instead retain
S_l=sum_k e_kl f_k f_k^T and contract
K_l(theta)=B(theta)^T S_l B(theta). This costs L m(m+1)/2 retained scalars,
plus B and contraction scratch, and is conditional on the exact feature identity
and envelope ownership. Alternative sparse contractions require their own proof
and byte accounting. None of these formulas awards additional free storage or
licenses dropping cross terms. Replaying evidence plus its derived posterior as
independent contributions is forbidden.

## 3. Why these bounds do not repair the full score by themselves

For direct residual r and candidate residual r+e at the same point, with
J=Dr and E=De and a common prior, expansion gives

    Delta g = J^T W e + E^T W r + E^T W e.

The frozen-J bound can control the additive term with a suitable declared design;
it does not dispose of E^T W r. For example, take W=I, scalar theta,
r(theta)=(theta,1), and e(theta)=(0,a theta), with a nonzero. At theta=0,
e=0 and J^T e=E^T e=0, but E^T r=a. The direct quadratic has minimizer
0 and candidate minimizer -a/(1+a^2). A zero pointwise residual error therefore
does not imply a zero score or fit displacement. The full nonlinear stationary
branch derivative is -H_exact^-1(J^T W e+E^T W r); J1 states its hypotheses.
A residual box alone does not control residual derivatives.

For joint precision, use direct P=Lambda+J^T W J and
Delta P=J^T W E+E^T W J+E^T W E. If
A=P^-1/2 Delta P P^-1/2 has norm at most eta<1, then

    (1-eta)P <= Phat <= (1+eta)P,
    P^-1/(1+eta) <= Phat^-1 <= P^-1/(1-eta).

Proof: whiten the first inequality and invert positive matrices. These are
same-anchor local GN covariance statements. Query covariance also requires the
candidate query derivative/transport error, as in P2; fit movement is separate.

No duration-uniform eta follows merely from bounded per-observation E. With
scalar parameter, W=I, fixed prior precision lambda>0, J=0 and
E=a times the n-vector of ones, P=lambda but Phat=lambda+n a^2 and
eta=n a^2/lambda. This weak-stimulus example refutes an unconditional cancellation
of sample count in relative precision. Informative-design lower bounds could
change the conclusion but must be proved uniformly for the admitted histories.

## 4. Support, nonlinear fits and uncertainty meanings

A box formed by estimate plus/minus four plug-in local SDs is a conditional
computational choice, not a deterministic bound on the unknown parameters.
Marginal Gaussian-looking widths do not prove joint support, calibrated coverage,
or containment after representation bias. A support-based envelope requires a
separately qualified joint region accounting for correlations, prior assumptions,
representation and numerical error. If its definition uses the covariance or
bias certificate being proved, establish a self-consistent enclosure (for example
a verified inclusion of the updated region in the region used for its bounds).
An unverified iteration or shrinkage of the public support does not do so.

For nonlinear optimization fix P0>0 and a convex box. If the EXACT Hessian obeys
||P0^-1/2(H_exact(theta)-P0)P0^-1/2|| <= omega < 1/2
throughout that box, then H_exact >= (1-omega)P0 > 0 there; integration
along segments proves strict convexity. The threshold 1/2 is sufficient, not
necessary. A bound only on GN precision does not establish this premise because
residual-weighted second derivatives remain.

Even exact strict convexity gives at most one stationary point, not its existence
or interior containment. On a compact box continuity gives a constrained minimum,
which may be on the boundary. Interior existence can additionally be certified,
for example, by strict outward normal gradient on every face (negative partial
on each lower face and positive on each upper face); a boundary minimizer then
has a feasible descent direction, so the minimizer is interior. These are extra
verified conditions. Once both fits and their connecting segment are certified,
P2's integrated exact-Hessian bound applies to the FULL score defect. None of
this follows solely from omega<1/2 at the computed fit.

An application error budget must separately bound measurement-noise effects,
prior bias, representation bias, nonlinear linearization remainder, arithmetic
error and query/forecast representation error. The mean forecast difference has
both a same-anchor transport defect and a parameter-shift contribution. A local
SD plus this packet's frozen-J bias is not automatically a physical confidence
interval. A probabilistic statement needs the specified noise/prior law and a
proof or validation of its coverage, including every conditioning event.

## 5. Model checks and long histories

Under independent correctly scaled Gaussian errors at a fixed correct mean,
the unfit standardized sum of squares has chi-square n distribution. After an
unregularized full-rank linear fit it instead has chi-square n-p distribution;
priors, nonlinear fits, constraints, estimated noise and correlated observations
change the law further. Therefore fitted SSR cannot generally be thresholded
as chi-square n. A model can agree on all observed stimuli and disagree on an
unexcited response or future load, so no residual test universally detects every
model inadequacy. Report stimulus support and the test's specific alternatives.

A five-sigma single-check tail, even when its assumed distribution is justified,
is not a lifetime alarm guarantee. For T checks with valid per-check false-alarm
bounds alpha_t, the union bound is sum_t alpha_t without independence. An
indefinite guarantee requires summable risk allocation or a valid sequential
construction; constant thresholds alone supply neither.

Finally, a duration-uniform physical response envelope is not a duration-uniform
likelihood, score or query guarantee. Coherent defects accumulate as signed bias,
not independent fresh noise. Moment statistics may have bounded storage while
their magnitudes, rounding errors, counters and conditioning grow with history.
Representation error and arithmetic error need separate ledgers and composition
proofs; aging additionally requires an evolution law with joint uncertainty.
No floating-point constants, ten-year numerical claims, or user-supplied
numerical estimates are accepted by these symbolic statements. No simulations,
new fits, estimator changes, or long sweeps were used to derive this packet.
