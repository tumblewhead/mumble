# D1: thermal and aging transport have their own dynamics

Scope: real arithmetic, known ordered input intervals, positive coefficients and
continuous piecewise smooth paths where derivatives are used. This packet
reconstructs the extension semantics; it does not certify a coefficient map,
aging law, uncertainty envelope, or target allocation.

## D1.1 Choose the physical thermal model before reducing time

The repo's voltage-state equation and a charge-continuity equation are different
when capacitance varies. If q=C(t)v and charge balance is qdot=I-q/(RC),
then differentiating gives

    vdot=I/C-v/(RC)-(Cdot/C)v.

The last term is absent from vdot=-v/tau+(R/tau)I. Thus a switch to charge
coordinates is not merely a code optimization unless this physical model change
is made explicit. Neither convention is silently selected here.

For the charge-continuity model, suppose R=Rref*r(t), tau=tauref*a(t), where
known r,a are positive, a is bounded away from zero, and define
vartheta(t)=integral_0^t ds/a(s). Then

    qdot=I-q/(tauref*a),  v=Rref*r*q/(tauref*a).

An integrating factor proves

    q(t)=q0*exp(-vartheta(t)/tauref)
         + integral_0^t exp(-(vartheta(t)-vartheta(s))/tauref)*I(s) ds.

Use parameter-independent bank charges Qj satisfying
Qjdot=I-Qj/(sigma_j*a) with zero initial state. The forced voltage approximation
is Rref*r(t)/a(t) * sum_j wj Qj/sigma_j. For initial physical voltage b,
the free part is b*[r(t)/a(t)]*[a(0)/r(0)]*Fhat(vartheta(t)). Keep b as an
unknown coefficient; do not hide it in a parameter-dependent bank initialization.

Let epsK bound integral_0^infinity |k-khat| in reduced-time units and epsF bound
|F-Fhat|, uniformly over the admitted reference tau. Changing variables
eta=vartheta(s), ds=a(s)deta, yields the explicit error bound

    |v-vhat| <= Rref*r(t)/a(t) * ||a I||_infinity * epsK
                + |b|*[r(t)/a(t)]*[a(0)/r(0)]*epsF.

The ratio and the changed input are essential. A stationary Rref*||I||*epsK
bound cannot be reused unchanged for arbitrary thermal histories. The same
argument applies to a reference log-tau derivative when a,r are independent of
that query and certified derivative bounds are available. This does not provide
an E_tau derivative certificate when the time warp itself changes with E_tau.
Frozen-query passivity likewise does not establish time-varying energy behavior.

## D1.2 Unknown activation energy and rebasing are approximate

On one interval assume I and T fixed, a=exp(E*g), ell=sigma*a, p=exp(-dt/ell).
Then Qnew=p Qold+ell(1-p)I is exact under these assumptions. Differentiation gives

    Snew=p Sold + p*dt*g/ell * Qold
         + [ell*g*(1-p)-p*dt*g]*I,    S=partial_E Q.

Both right-hand sides use OLD states. If T varies inside an interval, this
constant-coefficient step needs a qualified integration rule instead.

Moving a centre by Delta E and setting Qnewcentre=Q+Delta E*S has error bounded
by (Delta E)^2/2 times a uniform second-E derivative bound on that segment,
assuming the starting states and sensitivities are exact. Existing state errors
and sensitivity errors must also propagate. For an error injection d_k at a
rebase and subsequent linear state transitions p_i, induction gives

    e_n=(product_i p_i)e_0 + sum_k (product_(i>k) p_i)d_k.

Repeated local Taylor remainders do not disappear. This recurrence, including
sensitivity and numerical errors, is required before accepting a sequence of
moving centres. A final posterior radius alone does not bound every past rebase
or justify treating the retained state as an exact derivative at the new centre.

## D1.3 Endpoint drift features are not the aging convolution

For the voltage-state model with physical paths lambda(t)=1/tau(t)>0 and
B(t)=R(t)/tau(t), integrating factors give

    v(t)=b*exp(-integral_0^t lambda)
         + integral_0^t exp(-integral_s^t lambda) B(s)I(s) ds.

It depends on the entire path. Substituting endpoint R(t),u(t) in a stationary
bank readout is generally different. For example, with I=0 and
 tau(t)=tau0+alpha*t>0, the exact decay for alpha!=0 is

    F(t)=[tau0/(tau0+alpha*t)]^(1/alpha),

obtained by integrating 1/(tau0+alpha*t). The endpoint exponential
exp(-t/(tau0+alpha*t)) is different. This counterexample does not invalidate
slow-drift approximations; it identifies a missing dynamic remainder.

The proposed algebraic aging expansion itself also needs care. Let a be known
throughput, d=u-uhat, p=w(uhat)^T eta, p'=w'(uhat)^T eta and p'' similarly.
First-order expansion in d combined with second order in rho_u*a produces terms
from

    R*(1+rho_R*a)*[p+d*p' + rho_u*a*(p'+d*p'')
                           + (rho_u*a)^2*p''/2 + ...].

In particular a term R*rho_u*a*d*p'' is omitted by the four-feature expression
in the supplied handoff. An envelope may bound it, but the truncated feature list
is not the exact general second-order expansion in both d and drift. More
fundamentally this expansion describes a changing output map, not the above
path-dependent physical dynamics. The path discrepancy needs its own bound.

A useful conditional way to expose that discrepancy is to define, for ANY
absolutely continuous proposed response vhat,

    r_dyn=vdothat+lambda*vhat-B*I,  e=vhat-v.

Then edot=-lambda*e+r_dyn, hence the integrating-factor identity gives

    |e(t)| <= exp(-integral_0^t lambda)|e(0)|
              + integral_0^t exp(-integral_s^t lambda)|r_dyn(s)| ds.

If lambda>=lambda_min>0 and |r_dyn|<=D, this is at most
exp(-lambda_min*t)|e(0)| + D/lambda_min*(1-exp(-lambda_min*t)).
This is a conditional physical-response bound. Deriving useful D, its parameter
sensitivities, and cumulative likelihood consequences is still required. It is
not an artificial forgetting rule for evidence. Known input/thermal histories,
monotone aging support and coefficient bounds are assumptions, not simulator truth.

## D1.4 Interface to the full estimator

Our full model has three RC branches, SOC/charge integration, H, two unknown
hysteresis rates and uncertain initial hysteresis/RC states. Known single gamma
in the supplied study is a smaller conditional model. A valid integration must
retain the unknown-rate chronology and coupling to Q through charge throughput.
Neither one known h column nor a supplied activation-energy estimate discharges
those unknown quantities. Truth cannot set frozen feature centres or corrections.

An explicit deterministic aging law is one possible hypothesis. Its adequacy,
monotone parameter directions, discrepancy bounds and parameter uncertainty need
qualification; declaring process noise absent is not a proof of adequacy. No
thermal model or aging extension is adopted into production by this packet.
