# A1: physical state error and accumulated evidence error are different

This packet extends A7/S1 rather than choosing an aging law. Actual interval/order,
qualified inputs, boundary-state conventions, and unchanged ownership are premises.
All statements below are conditional on finite well-defined quantities.

## Affine state-error transport

For x_k=a_k*x_(k-1)+b_k and an approximation
xhat_k=ahat_k*xhat_(k-1)+bhat_k, define d_k=xhat_k-x_k. Direct subtraction gives

    d_k=ahat_k*d_(k-1)+(ahat_k-a_k)*x_(k-1)+(bhat_k-b_k).

If |ahat_k|<=rho_k and the absolute local defect at reference state is bounded
by epsilon_k, induction yields

    |d_N| <= product_(k=1..N)rho_k*|d_0|
              +sum_(j=1..N) epsilon_j*product_(l=j+1..N)rho_l.

With rho_k<=rho<1 and epsilon_k<=epsilon this is at most
rho^N*|d0|+epsilon*(1-rho^N)/(1-rho). Without a uniform contraction the displayed
finite product/sum remains valid; a duration-independent bound does not follow.
This is state-error transport, not a posterior or likelihood bound.

Under the selected continuous-voltage RC law dv/dt=-k(t)*v+k(t)*R(t)*I(t),
k(t)>0 integrable with the forcing integrable, the integrating factor gives

    v(t)=exp(-integral_0^t k)*v(0)
          +integral_0^t exp(-integral_s^t k)*k(s)*R(s)*I(s) ds.

Known temperature factors can enter k and R; unknown thermal calibration and
aging belong in the uncertain joint path. Equal final R,tau does not determine
these integrals. Nor does a final Q determine sum I*dt/(3600Q_k) under the
SOC-continuous piecewise-capacity convention. A present centroid cannot replace
the path without a sufficiency or bounded-approximation argument.

This chosen law is not forced by R(t),C(t) alone. If branch charge q=C*v is the
state and dq/dt=I-v/R, differentiating q gives

    dv/dt=I/C-v/(R*C)-(dC/dt)/C*v.

A charge-preserving jump in C changes v by C_before/C_after; a voltage-preserving
jump does not. The physical aging/thermal state convention must be selected or
bounded before either transport can be certified. Monotone R increasing and C
decreasing give no monotonic sign for tau=R*C. No fitted process noise or decay
factor is inferred from these identities.

## Positive-kernel evidence-error composition

Use the same initial measure, state spaces, support and chronological integration
law as S1. Let K_k be a nonnegative transition-evidence kernel and suppose its
approximation obeys, as measures for every admitted incoming state,

    exp(l_k)*K_k <= Khat_k <= exp(u_k)*K_k,

with finite constants l_k<=u_k independent of state. Positivity of integration
and induction give the unnormalized boundary-measure bound

    exp(sum l_k)*mu_N <= muhat_N <= exp(sum u_k)*mu_N.

Assume both total masses are finite and positive. Normalizing gives the
Radon-Nikodym density ratio bound on the shared support

    exp(-B_N) <= d p_hat_N / d p_N <= exp(B_N),
    B_N=sum_(k=1..N)(u_k-l_k).

Proof: the numerator ratio is within exp(sum l)..exp(sum u), and the ratio of
normalizers lies in the same interval; divide. State-independent scale errors
l_k=u_k cancel from the normalized result, though evidence normalizers change.
This bound is conservative and permits uncertainty/correlation across the whole
joint state. It needs no factorization of the posterior into physical partitions.

The kernel premise is substantive. Different deterministic state maps can give
mutually singular Dirac kernels, so a small state difference does not imply this
multiplicative bound. A separate coupling/reachability argument is needed there.
Likelihood-only approximations with unchanged transitions can meet the premise
when their log-likelihood error has a uniform bounded range on declared support.
For log error delta_k, choose l_k=inf delta_k and u_k=sup delta_k. Finite query
samples do not prove those extrema. Prior/observations must not be counted twice.

## Why physical relaxation does not certify indefinite history

Let an unknown constant label have two equally likely values, and let each fresh
observation have equal likelihood under the two labels. The exact posterior stays
1/2. An approximation giving likelihood ratio exp(epsilon) for label 1 on every
observation yields odds exp(N*epsilon). This is a coherent representation defect,
not physical evidence; for epsilon>0 it eventually dominates the posterior.
It coexists with a completely stable, contracting physical state recurrence.

Thus the generic evidence bound requires a finite-horizon cumulative budget,
summable errors, exact elimination of relevant defects, or a separately proved
filter-stability mechanism with its premises. Monotone aging, repetitive use,
a small state error or a fixed-size representation supplies none automatically.
Ten-year qualification remains open until the chosen joint evolution/measurement
law and actual compression admit useful quantitative bounds and availability.
