# O1: structure-preserving numerical reuse and coefficient compression

This packet proposes optimization acceptance relationships, not an optimized
kernel or resource improvement. It specializes reviewed K/A contracts. Exact real
arithmetic identities and finite-precision implementation bounds are distinct.

## Preserve equilibrium when approximating a decay

For one RC interval write x_next=a*x+(1-a)*b, with 0<=a<1 and equilibrium b=R*I.
If only a is approximated, and its complement is paired algebraically,

    xhat_next=ahat*xhat+(1-ahat)*b,
    d_next=ahat*d+(ahat-a)*(x-b).

Subtract the recurrences to prove the second equation. If 0<=ahat<=rho<1 and
|ahat-a|<=eta, the local forcing is at most eta*|x-b|. At x=xhat=b it is zero:
the equilibrium is exactly preserved in real arithmetic. For constant b,a,ahat,

    xhat_N-x_N=ahat^N*(xhat_0-b)-a^N*(x_0-b).

With equal initial states and 0<=a,ahat<1 the error therefore tends to zero,
although transient and accumulated likelihood errors need not vanish. The
reference and approximation must use the same b; errors in R/I are separate.

In contrast, independent additive errors in both coefficients give
xhat_next=(a+eta_a)*xhat+((1-a)+eta_b)*b. If |a+eta_a|<1 its fixed point is

    xhat_inf=b*((1-a)+eta_b)/(1-a-eta_a),
    xhat_inf-b=b*(eta_a+eta_b)/(1-a-eta_a).

This follows by solving the affine fixed-point equation. A small denominator
can amplify a small coefficient mismatch. The complementary condition
eta_b=-eta_a removes this equilibrium defect. Merely ensuring |a+eta_a|<1 does
not ensure unbiased equilibrium. If a quantizes to 1, contraction can be lost.

This motivates paired coefficient representation or an equilibrium-increment
form x_next=x+(1-a)*(b-x), but does NOT qualify replacing the existing exp/expm1
pair. Floating-point subtraction 1-exp(-dt/tau) can lose small increments;
expm1 is used in the current source for that reason. Separate rounding at the
multiply/add stages introduces additional errors even with paired coefficients.
An implementation must bound underflow, cancellation, arithmetic order, overflow,
and dt/rate range, and retain the finite transient/likelihood error budget.

## Exact coefficient reuse

In the stationary/reference RC recurrence a=exp(-dt/tau) is independent of I
and incoming state, but the new state is not. With thermal calibration a also
depends on T, the tau thermal law, its parameters and their versions. Reusing
a pure coefficient is exact only when all dependencies and numerical semantics
are identical. Reuse of an output state/observation row additionally depends on
incoming state, I, R thermal factors, sigma and the other row inputs. Hysteresis
decay further depends on current magnitude/direction and gamma/Q calibration.

Thus a registry can cache transition coefficients across different currents when
the required dependencies match, while state and evidence still advance for every
owned sample. A hash without collision handling, reused generation, omitted dt,
aging change or thermal calibration change does not establish that premise.
Storage readers, rollback and transaction semantics remain governed by A2-A4.
No cache hit rate, byte or cycle saving is asserted without a concrete profile.

## Compress a joint factor with query-aware error bounds

Let a frozen-prefix likelihood be L(theta)=1/2 ||R*phi(theta)||^2+c and let an
encoded factor be Rhat=R+DeltaR. The feature map and support are unchanged. At a
query define r=R*phi, e=DeltaR*phi, J=R*Dphi, and DeltaJ=DeltaR*Dphi. Expansion gives

    Lhat-L=r^T*e+1/2*e^T*e,
    ghat-g=J^T*e+DeltaJ^T*r+DeltaJ^T*e,
    |Lhat-L|<=||r||*||e||+1/2*||e||^2.

The factor encodes all joint cross terms: Rhat^T*Rhat remains positive
semidefinite, but can lose rank, distort precision or shift an optimum. PSD alone
is insufficient. An unchanged c has no query derivative; a changed constant must
be tracked for evidence normalization but cancels from a normalized posterior.

For a proved feature enclosure phi=phi0+L*z, ||z||<=1, a uniform residual-defect
bound follows from triangle inequality:

    ||DeltaR*phi|| <= ||DeltaR*phi0||+||DeltaR*L||_2.

This is a sufficient bound, not a claim that the nonlinear physical feature image
is exactly an ellipsoid. Derivative features need their own enclosure to bound
DeltaJ. Frobenius norm bounds the spectral norm if that is easier to certify.
The relevant metric for a codebook is its action on admitted values/derivatives
and application queries, not unweighted coefficient RMS. U1 explains the further
information normalization required in weak directions. The kernel-composition
premise in A1 is still needed to carry such bounds through changing-state history.
No quantizer, codebook size, exponent format or tolerance is selected here.
