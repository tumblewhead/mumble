# C1: shared Cayley query with a closed three-state RC response

Premises: the stationary reference-temperature Foster subfamily, positive fixed
reference tau_c, actual ordered nonnegative intervals, bounded piecewise constant
current and known positive sigma. Physical query t=log(tau/tau_c) retains the
original [-1,1] support. All identities here are exact arithmetic. This proof
does not supply full-support inference accuracy, positivity or lifetime rounding.

Define w=(tau-tau_c)/(tau+tau_c)=tanh(t/2), s=2w. Use the same linear functional
for both free and forced response, with derivatives at the fixed t=0 centre:

    L_t f=f(0)+s f_t(0)+(s^2/2) f_tt(0).

These are the existing three P1 streamed coefficients. Per-interval closure is
unchanged: F_new=e F, u_new=e u+(1-e)I and their first two log-tau derivatives,
using e=exp(-dt/tau_c). Product-rule induction proves those six free/forced states
are exact central jets for every finite prefix. Query weights do not affect their
streaming update or use future inputs. No event list, new pole or new state is added.

Since s(0)=0,s'(0)=1,s''(0)=0, L_t f matches the true response's value and first
two t derivatives at zero. Its query slope is s'*(f_t+s f_tt), where
s'=sech^2(t/2)=1-w^2. Apply that chain to every RC feature and physical parameter
mapping; the hysteresis/Q coupling and public prior are unchanged. Support is
checked on t, never s. Mapping to s is approximation, not a relabeling of the
physical rate or a reduction of the admitted parameter set.

## Physical identities and one joint factor

L_t is linear and reproduces constants. Under constant current the exact centre
jets obey u=I(1-F), u_t=-I F_t, u_tt=-I F_tt. Therefore
uhat=I(1-Fhat), including query derivatives, and b=RI remains exact equilibrium.
The same chronological centre-jet recurrence carries a forced response through
later rest; it is never reset. This is a shared kernel, unlike independent free5
and forced Taylor2 projections. Arbitrary-current consistency follows because
L_t commutes with a finite sum of fixed input steps and their responses.

Existing Chebyshev coefficient rows [f+f_tt/4,f_t,f_tt/4] dotted with
[1,T1(s),T2(s)] equal L_t f. Thus the entire observation row, response state and
joint QR factor can remain bitwise identical to the old Taylor RC/Taylor H field;
only the query feature map and derivative change. Floating implementations must
test that identity separately. Factor equality does not mean the likelihood
function is unchanged. The original physical/charge/hysteresis support and prior
must be retained, and full cross terms remain in one likelihood.

## Stationary transfer identity and error obligations

Let a=1/tau_c, d=a*time, lambda=1/tau=a(1-w)/(1+w). For the free response,

    Fhat(time;t)=exp(-d)*[1+d*s+(d^2-d)*s^2/2].

Let z denote the Laplace variable. Integrating the polynomial-exponential terms
gives Fhat_L=1/(z+a)+(2w-2w^2)a/(z+a)^2+4w^2 a^2/(z+a)^3.
Subtract from 1/(z+lambda) and put over a common denominator to obtain

    F_L-Fhat_L = 2a*w^3*(z-a)^2 /
                [(1+w)*(z+a)^3*(z+lambda)].

This is directly verifiable polynomial algebra, with no fit. The approximate
forced transfer is Ghat=1-z*Fhat_L, since Fhat(0)=1 and its time derivative
generates the shared impulse response. Consequently G-Ghat=-z*(F_L-Fhat_L),
the DC forcing gain stays one, and poles stay at the fixed stable -a. The three
states form a repeated-pole realization; internal stability is not positivity.

For k=-dF/dtime and khat=-dFhat/dtime, define
E0=||Fhat-F||_infinity, E1=||partial_t(Fhat-F)||_infinity,
K0=||khat-k||_L1(0,infinity), K1=||partial_t(khat-k)||_L1(0,infinity).
For each fixed query these are finite exponential-polynomial integrals/suprema.
They can be bounded uniformly on a compact t interval because decay rates stay
bounded away from zero, with polynomial-times-exponential dominating functions.
Then for every duration and |I|<=Imax,

    |vhat-v| <= |b| E0 + R Imax K0,
    |partial_t(vhat-v)| <= |b| E1 + R Imax K1.

This follows from convolution and differentiation under its integrable bound.
No numerical upper constants or useful certificate are established here. Bounded
response defects still do not imply bounded accumulated likelihood/score error;
J1/B1 and previous P3 remain obligations. No old Taylor-in-t remainder bound may
be applied directly to this different response function.

## Explicit physical limitation

At t=-1, put q=2*tanh(1/2)>2*(sqrt(2)-1). The free polynomial becomes
1-(q+q^2/2)d+(q^2/2)d^2. At d=1/q+1/2 its value is
1-(1+q/2)^2/2<0. Thus the candidate can have a negative free response inside the
original box. Its derivative may likewise violate monotone relaxation. Shared
equilibrium and stable poles do not certify positive/passive physical response.
This witness prohibits any unqualified physical-realization claim; it does not
erase the value of a bounded approximation comparison. No basis growth or support
narrowing is authorized as a repair in this study.
