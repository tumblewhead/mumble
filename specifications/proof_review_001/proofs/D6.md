# D6 — Certified numerical error accounting over time

## Premises

Exact state x_(k+1)=T_k(x_k) and computed state z_(k+1)=S_k(z_k) are compared
in one declared norm. Both lie in a domain where T_k has Lipschitz bound L_k>=0
and ||S_k(z)-T_k(z)||<=e_k. The bounds include response functions, features,
quantization, rounded kernels and all relevant error sources at that step. An
initial bound b_0 encloses ||z_0-x_0||. Domain/range/conditioning guards are valid.

## Exact and outward-rounded bound theorem

The triangle inequality gives

    ||z_(k+1)-x_(k+1)|| <= e_k+L_k||z_k-x_k||.

Induction proves containment by b_(k+1)=L_k b_k+e_k. Suppose instead the bound
engine stores nonnegative B_k>=b_k, upper constants Lbar_k>=L_k,
ebar_k>=e_k, and computes B_(k+1)>=Lbar_k B_k+ebar_k by a certified outward
operation. Positivity gives B_(k+1)>=L_k b_k+e_k=b_(k+1), so the bound engine
also preserves containment. This includes its own arithmetic error. Overflow
to an explicit infinite/unusable bound may preserve mathematical containment,
but cannot pass a finite error-budget predicate. Saturating to a smaller finite
number without a separate proof is invalid.

For uniform L_k<=rho<1 and e_k<=e, repeated substitution gives

    b_k <= rho^k b_0 + e(1-rho^k)/(1-rho).

Proof: induction on k, multiplying by rho and adding e, or summing the finite
geometric series. For L_k<=1, b_k<=b_0+sum_(i<k)e_i by direct induction.
SOC-like integrators cannot assume rho<1; quiescent hysteresis may also lack
strict contraction. Finite matrix/state dimension alone supplies neither bound.

## Error-budget authorization corollary

If a query has certified error <=L_f B_k+eta and its remaining implementation
budget is t, authorize on this basis only when L_f B_k+eta<=t and all premises
remain valid. Then its error meets t by substitution. A failed predicate may
trigger qualified recovery or unresolved status; it does not prove the point is
actually wrong. Reference physical/model uncertainty is an additional budget,
not eliminated by accurate computation of the reference.

A reset, re-centering or compressed re-expression can replace B_k only with a
new proved bound on the same required semantics. A small residual against the
compressed factor alone measures consistency with that factor, not agreement
with raw historical likelihood. Discarded observations cannot later reconstruct
their own approximation defect without a retained certificate. Deterministic
defects cannot silently be added as independent sensor variance.

## Scope

This proves how valid local bounds compose; it does not provide H7 constants or
prove those bounds stay below a useful threshold for ten years. The concrete
finite-horizon or invariant bounds for model approximation, library arithmetic,
factor accumulation, solves and the bound engine remain mandatory. A valid but
ever-growing bound ensures honest refusal, not useful sustained availability.
