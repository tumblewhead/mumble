# D2 — Query-specific authorization over a lifetime

## Assumptions

For one declared probability law, let a measurable event E have probability at
least 1-alpha, and suppose x_k in C_k simultaneously for every admitted decision
index k on E. Each queried scalar f_k has a verified enclosure [l_k,u_k] of
f_k(C_k), including approximation, transport and numerical evaluation. Endpoints
are finite and ordered; the set is nonempty. Queries and authorization times may
depend on observed data. Every issued certificate binds its actual quantity,
physical/source prefix, model configuration and validity horizon.

## Absolute and relative authorization theorem

Authorize a scalar q_k for absolute tolerance t_k>=0 only if

    max(|q_k-l_k|,|q_k-u_k|) <= t_k.

For any v in [l_k,u_k], convexity of |q_k-v| bounds it by the maximum endpoint
distance. On E the true query value is such a v, so every authorized output meets
its tolerance. Thus the event of any unsafe authorization is a subset of E^c,
and its probability is at most alpha. This proves the lifetime statement even
for selected times and multiple queries derived from the same simultaneous event.

For relative capacity error |q/Q-1|<=t with 0<l<=Q<=u and 0<=t<1, authorization
for all Q in the enclosure is equivalent to

    (1-t)u <= q <= (1+t)l.

Proof: the relative requirement equals (1-t)Q<=q<=(1+t)Q. Since both multipliers
are positive, intersecting these intervals over Q in [l,u] gives the displayed
extreme conditions. The feasible interval is nonempty exactly when
t>=(u-l)/(u+l). This is enclosure-relative necessity, not necessity relative to
a possibly much smaller true attainable range.

## Proposed-load corollary

Suppose E also ensures that the true future voltage trajectory for each
authorized action belongs to the admitted joint continuation set used in its
forecast. If a verified lower bound over that set and **every time** in the load
horizon is >=V_min plus the declared margin, the action satisfies that bound on E.
The same subset argument bounds any failure by alpha. Future sensor, actuator,
thermal and model disturbances must be included in this continuation premise.
Current-state containment alone does not bound unmodeled future disturbances;
additional probabilistic envelopes require joint risk accounting.

## Scope and selection caveat

This gives Pr(any unsafe authorization)<=alpha, not Pr(error | authorized)<=alpha.
Conditioning on rare selected authorizations can increase conditional error.
The guarantee is conditional on the validity of the simultaneous event and
enclosures, not on a residual monitor's silence. Pointwise Gaussian intervals
are not automatically simultaneous certificates. An invalid/stale/source-mismatched
certificate cannot be used. Validity at a stored prefix does not transfer to a
new prefix without proof of transport. No H7 certificate or physical coverage
law is constructed here; this theorem states what a valid one would authorize.
