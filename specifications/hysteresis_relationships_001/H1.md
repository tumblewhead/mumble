# H1: chronological exponential response and an exact segment algebra

This concerns the declared Foster hysteresis model, not a universal battery law.
Assume stationary positive Q,gamma_plus,gamma_minus, fixed reference temperature,
exact current/interval, and h in[-1,1]. Write k_s=gamma_s/Q with Q in Ah and
U=integral |I|dt/3600 in Ah. Then dh/dU=k_s(s-h) on a constant-sign segment.
At rest U is unchanged and the declared model has no hysteresis relaxation.
No exponential discount of statistical history is introduced here.

## Single-sign segment

For sign s in{-1,+1} and nonnegative throughput U, integration gives

    h_out = s + (h_in-s)*exp(-k_s U) = E*h_in + b,
    E=exp(-k_s U), b=s*(1-E).

Differentiating the solution verifies the ODE and its initial value. Thus only
throughput matters for the endpoint within a fixed-sign segment under these
premises; intermediate voltage likelihood still requires the observed chronology.
For 1-s*h>0, ell_s=log(1-s*h) satisfies

    ell_s(out)-ell_s(in) = -k_s U.

It linearizes the distance from the current direction's saturation. At saturation
that log coordinate is singular. A reversal requires
`ell_(-s)=log(2-exp(ell_s))`, which is nonlinear and may also be singular at the
opposite boundary. This is not permission to logarithmically transform noisy
voltage residuals or use two independent hysteresis estimators.

For adjacent segments/blocks represented by(E1,b1),(E2,b2), chronological
composition is

    (E2,b2) after (E1,b1) = (E2*E1, b2+E2*b1).

Substitution proves the identity; associativity follows by expanding three maps.
For 0<=E<=1, |b|<=1-E the map sends[-1,1] into[-1,1]. Composition preserves
these inequalities by |b2+E2*b1|<=1-E2+E2*(1-E1)=1-E2*E1.
A fixed-rate endpoint needs two numbers for this map; unknown-rate likelihood
history is a different object and is not reduced to two numbers by this lemma.

## Positive transport weights

For chronological segments j=1..M, let x_j=k_(s_j)*U_j, E_j=exp(-x_j), and
F_j=product over l>j of E_l. Expansion gives

    h_M = h_0*product E_j + sum_j s_j*(1-E_j)*F_j.

Define w0=product E_j, wplus=sum over s_j=+1 of(1-E_j)F_j and similarly wminus.
Each is nonnegative. Telescoping (1-E_j)F_j=F_j-product_(l>=j)E_l gives
w0+wplus+wminus=1. Hence h_M=w0*h0+wplus-wminus is bounded.
These are physical transport weights, not posterior probabilities over batteries.
They depend jointly on the rates and chronological exposures.

## Switch/exposure representation

Let Uplus,Uminus be the total directional throughputs, and let Uplus_>j,Uminus_>j
include only segments after j. Set

    E_all = exp(-kplus*Uplus-kminus*Uminus),
    E_tail_j = exp(-kplus*Uplus_>j-kminus*Uminus_>j).

For M>=1, telescoping the previous expression yields

    h_M = s_M + (h0-s_1)*E_all
                + sum_(j=1..M-1) (s_j-s_(j+1))*E_tail_j.

Terms with unchanged sign vanish. Rest has zero exposure and can be omitted from
this endpoint identity only. With B=H*h0, the voltage contribution is

    H*h_M = B*E_all + H*[s_M-s_1*E_all
                           +sum_j(s_j-s_(j+1))*E_tail_j].

Thus the free response E_all is exactly separable in the two rates; the forced
response is a signed sum of exponentials at suffix-exposure points. Each
exponential uses the scalar dose kplus*a+kminus*b, but different reversals have
different(a,b). The suffix points lie on a monotone axis-aligned throughput path.
This is the specific geometry behind the two-rate coupling. Cancellation in the
signed form makes stable affine/positive-weight evaluation preferable to naive
subtraction; it does not justify deleting reversal terms.

Chronology is essential. With a=exp(-kplus*Uplus), b=exp(-kminus*Uminus), a plus
segment followed by minus differs from the reverse order by

    h_(plus,minus) - h_(minus,plus) = -2*(1-a)*(1-b).

This follows from composing the two affine maps and is independent of h0. Equal
total positive/negative throughput therefore cannot in general summarize the
forced response. No claim is made that all possible nonlinear coordinate maps
are excluded; this counterexample excludes an endpoint determined by totals alone.

## Scope and relation to earlier work

The earlier response_symbolic_diagnostic_001 already derived the free/forced
exponential sums. Its rates multiply Ah directly; in the present augmented model
those rates are k=gamma/Q, not bare gamma. The new use here is the explicit
segment/switch algebra, order counterexample, cycle geometry(H2) and estimation
projection(H3). The bounded-exponential posterior natural parameters in
natural_hysteresis_001 describe a different object and are not these transport
weights/rates.

Known temperature factors with stationary reference k can be folded into
U_s=integral |I| f_gamma_s(T)/f_Q(T) dt/3600 on each sign. All exposures must then
use those factors. Unknown calibration/temperature and time-varying Q/gamma require
joint uncertainty/path integration; substituting today's k into old raw Ah is
not valid. Aging and physical calibration are unqualified. Changing H also changes
the relation between voltage and h. No global fixed-size history or firmware
implementation is established by these endpoint identities.
