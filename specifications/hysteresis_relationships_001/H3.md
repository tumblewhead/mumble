# H3: hysteresis error projects onto charge moments and fitted capacity

Use the stationary exact-input linear-OCV family and independent coordinates
(amplitudes,kplus,kminus,tau1,tau2,tau3), with q=1/Q, B=H*h0. OCV slope m>0
and signed charge A_i=sum_(j<=i) I_j*dt_j/3600 give

    V_i = c + m*(z0-q*A_i) + other_RC_ohmic_terms_i + H*u_i + B*E_i.

Weights w_i=1/sigma_i^2 are fixed positive public observation weights, independent
of all queried parameters. Fix shapes and all other amplitudes for the following
conditional statements. Let e_i=H*(u_hat_i-u_i)+B*(E_hat_i-E_i) be the unwhitened
hysteresis voltage-response defect (approximate minus reference). It is independent
of z0,q in these coordinates. Changing q at fixed bare gamma would change k and
would require additional derivative terms: the formulas below must not be reused
in that chart without its chain rule.

## Exact charge-moment gradient identities

For L=1/2 sum w_i(V_i-y_i)^2, the z0 and q Jacobian columns are exactly m and
-m*A_i. Their approximation defects are zero. Direct expansion gives

    g_hat_z0-g_z0 = m * sum_i w_i e_i,
    g_hat_q-g_q = -m * sum_i w_i A_i e_i.

These hold at any admitted query, not only a stationary fit. They explain why a
small systematic hysteresis response error can alter SOC/capacity inference:
its weighted mean and signed-charge correlation matter, not just its RMS.
For the full joint gradient, the already reviewed attribution identity retains
all other terms `J^T dr + dJ^T r + dJ^T dr`; independent fits/diagonal uncertainty
are not justified by these two projections.

## Conditional two-parameter fit displacement

Assume both fits minimize the unconstrained weighted quadratic in z0,q, with the
same fixed nuisance amplitudes/shapes, no changing prior/regularizer and
Var_w(A)>0. Let weighted means, covariance and variance be normalized by sum w.
If delta_z,delta_q are approximate optimum minus reference optimum, stationarity
of their difference gives

    sum w*(m*delta_z-m*A*delta_q+e)=0,
    sum w*A*(m*delta_z-m*A*delta_q+e)=0.

Eliminate delta_z to obtain

    delta_q = Cov_w(A,e)/(m*Var_w(A)),
    delta_z = -mean_w(e)/m + mean_w(A)*delta_q.

This is an exact difference of these two unconstrained conditional quadratic
optima, not a first-order expansion. The data residuals cancel from the normal
equations; no assumption that the reference fit equals truth is required.
If q and q+delta_q are positive, then

    (Q_hat-Q_ref)/Q_ref = -delta_q/(q+delta_q).

For small displacement it is approximately-delta_q/q. An inactive physical
constraint leaves the same solution; active support constraints, an added prior,
unknown shapes/nuisances, nonlinear OCV, input errors, aging or different objectives
invalidate this simple fit formula unless explicitly re-derived. A conditional
unconstrained optimum may be physically inadmissible; it must not be published
as a battery estimate on this identity alone.

At an endpoint A_N, delta_z_N=delta_z-A_N*delta_q under this SOC convention.
A constant e shifts z0 by-e/m and does not shift q when Var_w(A)>0. A defect e=b*A
shifts q by b/m and leaves z0 unchanged. With no variation in signed charge,
Var_w(A)=0 and this joint intercept/capacity fit is rank-deficient; the formula
must be reported unresolved rather than divided by zero. Rest does not create
capacity identifiability merely because an implementation returns a narrow interval.

## Bounds and why coherent errors do not average away

Weighted Cauchy-Schwarz gives

    |delta_q| <= SD_w(e)/(m*SD_w(A)).

This is a conditional fit-displacement bound, not a coverage statement. Small
charge variation weakens it. If |delta_q|<=b<q, then relative capacity displacement
is at most b/(q-b). To ensure a tolerance t in this particular conditional
comparison, it suffices that b<=t*q/(1+t). Neither this inequality nor the existing
1% development objective selects a physical error envelope for a real cell.

For N repetitions with an identical fixed full-rank conditional design and error
pattern, both precision and gradient defect multiply by N, so this conditional
fit displacement stays constant. Under the matching independent Gaussian noise
model the formal conditional standard deviations of the linear(z0,q) fit fall
as1/sqrt(N), making a fixed nonzero displacement grow as sqrt(N) in those SD
units. For Q=1/q this transfers only as a local delta-method precision statement,
not a claim about exact reciprocal-normal moments. This is a controlled
repeated-design example. Actual charge trajectories, nuisance uncertainty and
correlated noise need not obey its premises. A constant voltage bias can be fully
absorbed by an intercept, so the capacity component can be zero.

This supplies a symbolic route to a representation acceptance criterion: preserve
or bound response error projected against the physically relevant joint Jacobian
columns, including charge moments, as well as response/transport and curvature.
Do not preserve only the two moments above and discard the remaining joint
likelihood. Unknown rates, moving support and query selection still need A5/A6
coverage/domain/error arguments. No scalar NLL threshold, forgetting factor,
empirical uncertainty inflation or new production algorithm is introduced.
