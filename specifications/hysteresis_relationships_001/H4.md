# H4: the omitted mixed quadratic term predicts centre response error

Consider either exact forced response u(tplus,tminus) or free response E at one
observation boundary, with the other quantities fixed. This lemma concerns the
specific R2/H2/log collocation basis, not every degree2 approximation. In each
normalized log-rate coordinate its three Chebyshev nodes are
sqrt(3)/2,0,-sqrt(3)/2. The full tensor interpolant P has coefficients c_ij,
0<=i,j<=2, in T_i(tplus)T_j(tminus).

The current total-degree2 projection keeps i+j<=2, omitting(1,2),(2,1),(2,2).
On this three-by-three grid the Chebyshev columns are discretely orthogonal:
in one dimension the vectors for T0,T1,T2 are (1,1,1),
(sqrt(3)/2,0,-sqrt(3)/2),(1/2,-1,1/2), whose distinct dot products vanish.
Their tensor products inherit orthogonality by factoring the grid sum.
Consequently the least-squares projection onto the six retained columns has
exactly the corresponding six coefficients of the full nine-term interpolant.
This is an exact-arithmetic statement; floating-point QR/pseudoinverse/node
evaluation introduce separately measured rounding effects.

At tplus=tminus=0, T1=0,T2=-1. Hence

    P_TD2(0,0)-F(0,0) = -c22,

because P interpolates the exact central node and the two omitted odd terms
vanish there. In particular, for H*u+B*E at fixed public-centre rates,

    e_hysteresis = -H*c22(u) - B*c22(E).

It holds for any finite ordered input history represented by the exact nodal
recurrences, not only a two-run or repeated cycle. This identity is about the
single centre point; it is not a uniform bound over the box or a fitted-accuracy
claim. At arbitrary t the exact difference from the full tensor interpolant is

    P_TD2-P = -c12*T1(tplus)*T2(tminus)
                -c21*T2(tplus)*T1(tminus)
                -c22*T2(tplus)*T2(tminus).

At the centre its plus/minus derivatives are c12 and c21 respectively. These
are derivative differences from P, not from the exact physical response: full
tensor interpolation does not generally reproduce the true derivative at nodes.
Restoring c22 alone fixes the centre response value but cannot fix both central
derivative differences or the remaining between-node approximation.

## Explicit mixed-curvature relationship

The univariate quadratic coefficient is

    c2(f) = [f(+a)-2*f(0)+f(-a)]/3,  a=sqrt(3)/2.

Applying this in both coordinates gives

    c22(F) = [sum four corners -2*sum four edge midpoints +4*F(0,0)]/9.

This is a mixed finite difference on the collocation grid. For the exactly
separable free response E=f(tplus)*g(tminus), c22(E)=c2(f)*c2(g).
If the history uses only one current direction, u and E are independent of the
unused rate, so their c22 vanish. The TD2 centre value is then exact up to
roundoff, although its rate derivative and off-centre values need not be exact.

For exactly two sign runs, with s1 the first direction and exponentials Eplus,
Eminus for their throughputs, H1 gives a forced response equal to a constant,
a univariate exponential and -s1*Eplus*Eminus. Thus

    c22(u)=-s1*c2(Eplus)*c2(Eminus),
    e_hysteresis=(H*s1-B)*c2(Eplus)*c2(Eminus).

The sign of each univariate c2 can vary with exposure in log-rate coordinates;
the product and order therefore matter. A small-dose curvature sign or a single
coordinate preference is not a universal statement about longer histories.

## Consequence for the next controlled comparison

Keeping c22 for each of forced/free H responses would increase their basis size
from6to7, total lifted width from34to36 and packed joint factor from4760to5328B
(+568B). Its existing three-by-three nodal response state could be reused; the
numeric factor+response+domain/scalar subtotal would be5736B, before tables,
metadata, prior, solver, all scratch and platform memory. This is an accounting
estimate and a narrowly targeted hypothesis, not an implemented/promoted repair.
The two omitted odd terms still matter to derivatives/off-centre queries.

Keeping the full3x3H tensor would instead have width40 and factor6560B, with
lower numeric subtotal6968B using the same312B response state and96B domain/scalars.
It removes all three truncation terms relative to P but retains interpolation
error relative to the physical function. Those subtotals are smaller than the
7224B factor /7744B lower subtotal of H3 total-degree with a4x4node grid; this
does not establish that they are more accurate everywhere or fit the total target.

Any intervention must keep the one joint square-root likelihood, unchanged
support/prior and evidence ownership. Test value, derivative and application-query
effects separately, including the previous regressions; do not declare a solved
history compressor from central-node exactness. H1/H3 connect this missing response
term to charge-moment gradient defects without assuming a posterior factorization.
